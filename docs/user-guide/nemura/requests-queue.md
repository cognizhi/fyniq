# Decide changes of details and answer questions

## What it is

**Requests** lists what waits for the people team: changes of legal name, residency or NRIC or FIN that people asked for with evidence, and questions to the people
team, soonest due first. Nobody decides their own change or answers their own question: another member of the people team does.

## Before you start

- You are an HR officer, an HR administrator or the Owner.

## Steps

1. Open **Requests**. Each row names the person, what they ask and the day it is due.
2. For a change of details, select **Read the evidence** and check it against what the person wrote.
3. To approve, select **Approve** for that person. To refuse, write the reason under **Reason for refusing** and select **Refuse**; a refusal needs a reason, which the person sees.
4. For a question, write the answer under **Answer to** that person and select **Send the answer**.

## What happens next

- Approving a legal name changes the person's record from the day you decide. Approving a residency starts a new legal classification from that day, with the day
  permanent residence began for a permanent resident.
- Approving an NRIC or FIN change records only that the evidence was checked. Then record the new number under the person's restricted fields; the request never held it.
- A decided request keeps its words and its evidence and is decided once; the audit trail names who decided and why, never the person's details.

## Common problems

- **Nemura says nobody decides their own change.** Ask another member of the people team.
- **The person has no legal classification.** Record it first, then approve a residency change.
- **A decision is refused as decided.** Someone decided it first: reload the list.
