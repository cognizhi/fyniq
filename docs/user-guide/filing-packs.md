# Year-end tax working paper

## What it is

**Compliance › Year-end tax** works out, for the tax agent, a financial year's corporate income tax from the books: the profit before tax and the revenue come from the ledger, the
adjustments come from you with their reasons, and the exemption, the rate and the rebate of the Year of Assessment come from the statutory packs. It files nothing and keeps nothing:
it is a calculation the tax agent signs.

## Work out a year

1. Enter the day the financial year ends, which must be in a month the books hold.
2. Choose the exemption: the partial tax exemption, or the start-up tax exemption for a qualifying company's first three Years of Assessment.
3. Tick the cash grant box only if the company met its local employee condition.
4. Select **Add an adjustment** for each amount to add back (an expense the Act does not allow) or to take off (the capital allowances over the depreciation, entered negative), with the reason.
5. Select **Work it out**. The paper shows each step, the tax payable rounded once at the end, and the form the revenue calls for: Form C-S (Lite) up to S$200,000 of revenue, Form C-S up to
   S$5 million, otherwise Form C. A company within the Lite limit may file Form C-S too.

A Year of Assessment with no announced rebate has none. The accountant confirms how the tax is rounded.

## Statutory updates

When a newer version of a statutory pack you have activated is in force, the page lists it with the rows it would add, remove or change, so you see what accepting it does. Accepting a
pack is done in Settings › Statutory packs, by two people. A correction to a past period never re-posts anything by itself.
