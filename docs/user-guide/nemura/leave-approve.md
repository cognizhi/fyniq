# Approve or refuse leave

## What it is

**Team leave** lists the leave requests waiting for your decision and the ones decided lately. A line manager sees and
decides on their reports' requests. The HR officer, the HR administrator and the Owner see and decide on anyone's. The
Payroll administrator can read them. Nobody decides their own request: another approver does.

## Before you start

- You need to be a line manager, the HR officer, the HR administrator or the Owner.
- Your reports are the people whose manager you are on the reporting line.

## Steps

1. Open **Team leave**.
2. Under **Waiting for a decision**, read the person, the dates, the days and the note.
3. Select **Approve**. To refuse, write the reason in the box beside the buttons first, then select **Refuse**. A refusal
   without a reason is not accepted: the person is told why.
4. The decision is audited, the person is told, and a refusal gives the reserved days back to their balance.

## The month at a glance

Under the requests, **The month at a glance** shows a row for each person you may see and a column for each day. A letter marks
the day: **A** away, **L** leave, **P** a request waiting, **S** sick leave and **H** a public holiday as that person observes
it (a holiday on a rest day moves to their next working day). A dot is a rest day. You see the type of leave for your reports,
and for everyone if you are in the people team; for the others in your team the calendar says **Away** and nothing more. Sick
leave is shown as such only to the person and to the HR officer and the HR administrator.

## Verify medical certificates

The HR officer and the HR administrator also see **Certificates to verify**. Select **Verify** once you have checked a
certificate. It is verified once, and never by the person it is about. A manager cannot refuse a certified sick day the person is
entitled to, and does not see the certificate.

## Answer flexible work requests

The HR officer, the HR administrator and the Owner also see **Flexible work requests**: each request waiting for its written
reply, soonest deadline first. Choose **accepted**, **refused** or **an alternative was offered**, write the reply, and select
**Send reply**. A refusal or an alternative gives the business grounds. The reply is kept as the record the guidelines ask the
employer to keep, and the person is told. You cannot answer your own request.

## Hours worked, sick days beyond the limit and the pay run

- **Hours waiting for approval** lists the overtime, rest-day and public-holiday hours your reports entered, and for the people team
  everyone's. Select **Approve**, or write a reason and select **Refuse**. Nobody approves their own hours. A day flagged **Beyond
  the monthly limit** stays recorded and is paid: apply for an overtime exemption or change the person's working pattern.
- **Sick days beyond the paid limit** (the HR officer and the HR administrator): for each notification with days beyond the paid
  limit, choose **Unpaid leave** or **Take from annual leave**. It is decided once.
- **What the next pay run takes from leave and time** lists unpaid leave, encashed annual leave and approved hours not yet used
  by a pay run, each linked to its source.
- **Month-end leave accrual**: choose a month, then **Draft the month** (the HR officer and the HR administrator) to list the
  carried-forward annual leave still untaken for each person, and **Approve the month** (not by the person who drafted it). Nemura
  also drafts the previous month by itself while Leave is on. The amounts and the journal come from payroll and the Fyniq link.

## Common problems

- **A request is not in the list.** You see only the requests of the people who report to you, unless you are in the
  people team.
- **It says the request is approved already.** A decision is made once. To change it, the person cancels the request, or
  the people team corrects it.
- **You cannot decide your own request.** Ask another approver.
