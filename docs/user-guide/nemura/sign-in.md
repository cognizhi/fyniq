# Sign in to Nemura

## What it is

You sign in to Nemura with the account your company set up for you. Nemura keeps its own sessions,
so it works even when your company does not use Fyniq. It never asks for your NRIC to sign in.

## Before you start

- Wait for your invitation. Your company sends it; you open it after you sign in.
- Nemura must be set up. If the page says **Nemura is not set up yet**, the person who installs it
  has not created the first organisation, so there is nothing to sign in to yet.

## Steps

1. Open Nemura's address.
2. Select **Continue with** the provider your company uses.
3. Sign in at the provider. You come back to Nemura on your own home page: **My home**, or **People
   home** if you are in the people team.

## Joining with a code

- **I have an invitation**: choose it on the sign-in page, pick the provider, type the invitation code and
  continue. You sign in with the provider, and the code links that account to the place your company made
  for you. It works once and expires.
- **Set up Nemura**: only the person who installs Nemura does this, once. It needs the one-time setup code
  the installation prints. You give the organisation's name and your own, sign in with the provider, and you
  become the first organisation's Owner.
- **I have a recovery code**: for an Owner who can no longer sign in. Two operators of the installation
  issue the code (valid 24 hours, works once). Sign in with the account you will keep, type the code, and
  every other sign-in, session and passkey of yours ends. Register a passkey again afterwards.
- The code is never in the address bar. If it is wrong, used already or expired, you land back on sign-in
  with a message; ask for a new one.

## Moving between Nemura and Fyniq

When your company links Nemura to Fyniq and you hold a role in Fyniq's finance workspace, a **Finance | People** switch shows in the header. Fyniq tells Nemura, signed, that the account you signed in with holds such a role, and nothing more: no role name, no email. The switch is a door and never a grant: Fyniq's own sign-in and roles decide what you see there, and a role in one app gives you nothing in the other. It opens Fyniq with the same sign-in provider you used here, so you are not shown a sign-in page again; if your provider asks you to confirm, do so. Nemura and Fyniq use one sign-in provider for your company: use the same account in both. If the switch does not show, Fyniq has not said you hold a finance role, so ask the Owner.

## Signing out

Select **Sign out** at the top of any page. It ends the session on this device only; other devices stay
signed in until you sign out on them.

## Common problems

- **No way to sign in is set up yet.** Nobody has connected a sign-in provider. Ask whoever runs Nemura
  for your company.
- **We could not reach Nemura.** Check your connection and select **Try again**.
- **Your account is not a member of any organisation here.** Ask your company for an invitation, then choose
  **I have an invitation**.
- You are sent back to sign-in after a while: the session ended. Sign in again.
