# Record a filing, or that it is not required

## Steps

1. On the item in **Compliance › Calendar** ([[compliance.calendar]]), select **Record as filed**.
2. Enter the day you filed or paid, and the acknowledgement or transaction number.
3. Attach the acknowledgement: IRAS's, CPF Board's or ACRA's, or the AGM's minutes.
4. Select **Record**: the item shows as filed and leaves the reminders and Checks.

(src: docs/user-guide/statutory-calendar.md#record-a-filing)

## Not required

- Some items need nothing in a year: for example, ECI is waived when the year's revenue is $5 million or below and the ECI is nil.
- Select **Not required**, say why, and select **Record**; no file is asked for.

(src: docs/user-guide/statutory-calendar.md#not-required)

## Withdraw a record

- A record is never changed: select **Withdraw**, say why, and record the item again.
- The item is open again, and both records stay in its history.

(src: docs/user-guide/statutory-calendar.md#withdraw-a-record)
