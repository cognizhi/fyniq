# The corporate profile and registers

## What it is

- **Compliance → Corporate profile and registers** keeps the company as ACRA's registers hold it: its name, registered
  office and SSIC code, its officers, the registers of nominee directors and nominee shareholders, and the directors'
  approval of each year's financial statements.
- The UEN and the financial years stay in the organisation's settings; the registrable controllers stay in the equity
  register.
- The Accountant and the Company secretary record changes. The Owner and the Controller approve them. The Auditor, the
  Tax agent and the Board viewer can see the profile, and the DPO sees it with identifiers hidden.

## Record a change

- Each record is a **draft** first. Attach its evidence, such as the BizFile+ acknowledgement, the officer's consent to
  act or the directors' resolution, then choose **Ask for approval**.
- Another person approves it, never the one who asked, and never the officer or nominee it is about.
- A recorded record never changes. To record a cessation, the day a change was lodged or a correction, record a change
  of it: the new version replaces the old one, which stays in the history.
- Only the last 4 characters of an officer's identifier are kept. The full number stays with ACRA and the identity
  document.

## The company and its officers

- Record the company's details from incorporation first: the checks count from that day.
- A change of the registered office is lodged with ACRA within 14 days, and so is each officer's appointment or
  cessation.
- A director, the company secretary and the CEO are people; an auditor can be a firm, by its UEN.
- The company secretary is marked as the **named secretary**. A company needs one within six months of incorporation,
  and the sole director cannot also be the secretary.

## Nominee directors and shareholders

- Record each nominee director or nominee shareholder, whom they act for, and since when.
- A nominee tells the company within 30 days. The company enters them in its register within 7 days, then tells ACRA's
  central register within 2 business days after.

## Checked against ACRA

- Type in ACRA's business profile, with its officers and registered office, under **Equity → Reconciliation**.
- The officers in office and the registered office are compared with it on its day. Each difference shows, and is
  fixed in the profile or by typing in a newer business profile.
- With no business profile, none with its officers typed in, or one older than the last change recorded, the
  comparison is **Not assessed**.

## Draft a resolution, notice or minutes

- Fyniq drafts the company's papers from its own templates, filled from the records on the day you draft them: the
  directors' resolutions approving the financial statements, allotting shares, appointing an officer, ending the
  secretary's or CEO's appointment or changing the registered office; a members' written resolution; the AGM's notice
  and its minutes; and the notice that the financial statements were sent without an AGM.
- Choose the kind of paper, fill in what it asks for, and read the draft. Download it as PDF, have it signed outside
  Fyniq, and keep the signed copy.
- Fyniq never signs or files a draft, and each draft says so at its top.
- The templates are Fyniq's, and a lawyer has yet to confirm them. Read the wording with the company secretary before
  anyone signs.
- A directors' resolution needs the directors in office recorded, a members' resolution the shares issued in the
  equity register, and a notice the company secretary.
- Fyniq does not yet check a notice's period, a written resolution's majority or when minutes go in the minute book.

## Lodgements and checks

- **Lodgements** lists each change ACRA must be told of, with its deadline. It is done once a version with the day
  lodged is recorded.
- Four checks run without the AI assistant: the company has its secretary, the directors approve the financial
  statements within 30 days of their deadline, the profile agrees with ACRA, and every change is lodged on time.
- A check that lacks what it needs is **Not assessed** and names it, never all clear.
