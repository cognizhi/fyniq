# Threat model: the equity register

| | |
|---|---|
| Feature and requirement IDs | The equity register's holders and share classes: FR-EQ-15, FR-EQ-16, CMP-PD-07 (PRD-001 rev 0.203, rev 0.204); `p1-equity-register` slice 1. Equity transactions: FR-EQ-17, INV-13, FM-9 (rev 0.205), slice 2a; their ACRA steps and money from the bank (rev 0.206), slice 2b. The cap table: FR-EQ-20, slice 3. SAFEs, convertible notes and their templates: FR-EQ-18, slice 4a; their conversion at a priced round, slice 4b. Later slices (the ACRA steps and bank matching, the cap table, instruments, options, rounds, controllers, the ACRA reconciliation) extend this model. |
| Author, reviewer, date | Claude Code for the founders, 7 Nov 2026; reviewer: a founder at sign-off (second human review waived before production, AGENTS.md) |
| Data and its class | Holders' identity: an individual's identifier type and last 4 characters, nationality, address and contact people (personal data); identity documents (data class identity); share classes and their rights (confidential); the register of share issues |
| Who acts | The Accountant drafts (C/P); an Owner or the Controller approves (A); the Auditor and Tax agent read; the DPO reads masked; Employees and the Board viewer list no holders until their own holding and grants arrive with the cap table |

## 1. What we are building

A holder is a contact marked shareholder or investor. Its details are a holder record document, drafted with its
identity document as evidence and recorded on another person's approval. A share class is a document of its rights,
recorded the same way. Trust boundaries:
- the preparer → API: a record is hostile input;
- the API → the approval engine: a second person decides;
- the API → readers: personal data is shown by role;
- the evidence store → readers: an identity document.

## 2. What can go wrong (STRIDE per boundary)

| # | Boundary or step | Threat (STRIDE) | Impact | Likelihood |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | preparer → API | Information disclosure: a full NRIC or FIN stored as the identifier, in the address or in a note | Excess personal data held (PDPA, NRIC guidance) | Medium |
| E2 | preparer → API | Tampering: a record written straight to the database with extra keys, bypassing the API's checks | The rules above are bypassed | Low |
| E3 | approval | Elevation: the preparer, or the holder themselves, records their own details | A holder changes their own record | Medium |
| E4 | readers | Information disclosure: the DPO, an Employee or the Board viewer reads every holder's details, or opens an identity document | Personal data of every investor exposed | Medium |
| E5 | share issues | Tampering: an issue names a class of another account, or none once the account has classes | The cap table counts shares in the wrong class | Low |
| E6 | records | Repudiation: a recorded record or class is changed in place | History lost; no trail | Low |
| E7 | transactions | Elevation: a holder approves, or records effective, a transaction that gives them shares | A founder allots or transfers shares to themselves | Medium |
| E8 | transactions | Tampering: shares appear in a holding with no approved transaction, a transfer creates shares, or a holder gives away shares they do not hold | The cap table no longer matches the law | Low |
| E9 | transactions | Tampering: a cash issue is made effective before it is paid, or share capital moves outside the register | Capital stated that was never paid | Low |
| E10 | approvals | Elevation: the Accountant's new reach into the approvals lets them approve what they should only review | The review becomes a second approval by the wrong person | Low |
| E11 | ACRA steps | Repudiation: a lodgement date is changed after the fact, or recorded for a transaction it does not belong to | A missed statutory deadline is hidden | Low |
| E12 | bank → 2040 | Tampering: money in or a refund out is put on 2040 against no one, or against a customer, so a cash issue looks paid | Shares allotted against another person's money | Medium |
| E13 | cap table | Information disclosure: the Board viewer or an Employee reads every holder's holding, or another member's | Investors' holdings exposed | Medium |
| E14 | cap table export | Repudiation: the cap table leaves the company as a file with no record of who took it, when, or what it held | An unexplained copy of the register circulates | Low |
| E15 | instruments | Elevation: an investor's member records their own SAFE, or one is recorded with no signed copy, so the money it brings and the shares it converts into rest on nothing | Dilution nobody approved | Medium |
| E17 | 2700 | Tampering: money for a SAFE is put on 2700 against no instrument, or against one never recorded, so what each SAFE holds, and the shares it converts into, cannot be told apart | A SAFE converts for money it never brought | Medium |
| E16 | templates | Tampering: a template is enabled without a lawyer's confirmation, or an unknown form is made to convert by itself | SAFEs convert on terms nobody checked | Medium |
| E19 | a founder's SAFE | Tampering: a founder's balance is moved into a SAFE someone else holds, or past the SAFE's amount, so 2700 holds more for it than was ever agreed | A SAFE converts for money it was never owed | Medium |
| E20 | option pool | Tampering or elevation: a grant is recorded beyond what the pool leaves, on its date or a later one, two grants recorded at once both take its last options, a resolution shrinks the pool below the grants, or a holder approves their own grant | Options granted that no resolution set aside, so the fully diluted cap table overstates what the board approved | Medium |
| E22 | grant events | Tampering or elevation: a forfeiture or expiry takes more options than a grant has, a leaver forfeits options already vested, or the holder records their own, so options vanish or the pool grows past what the board set aside | A holder loses vested options, or the pool is granted twice | Medium |
| E23 | exercises | Tampering or elevation: an exercise issues shares past what is vested, at another price, to another holder, unpaid, or twice for the same options, or the holder approves their own | Shares issued that the plan never allowed, or for less than its price | Medium |
| E24 | round scenarios | Tampering or elevation: a modelled round posts, changes the register, or drafts issues nobody approved, or its outcome is stored and shown stale as if it were the books | Shares allotted from a sandbox, or a decision on figures that no longer hold | Low |
| E25 | investor rights | Tampering or elevation: an investor's rights are recorded without the agreement, by the investor themselves, or changed after they were recorded, so a board majority or consent rights go unseen | A controller nobody tested, or a pro-rata right overlooked in a round | Medium |
| E26 | registrable controllers and exempt status | Repudiation or tampering: the controller test says all clear while rights that could confer control are unweighed, a controller's entry is backdated or recorded by the controller, or the exempt status recorded stays though an entity joined | A late filing with ACRA, or loans to directors under the wrong rule (s162) | Medium |
| E27 | ACRA reconciliation | Tampering or repudiation: an extract is typed to match the register and hide a real difference, changed after it was recorded, or recorded with no business profile behind it, so the register drifts from ACRA's unseen | A register of members that differs from the statutory one | Medium |
| E21 | own grants | Information disclosure: an Employee reads another person's grants, or the whole pool | Colleagues' pay in options exposed | Medium |
| E18 | conversions | Tampering: a SAFE converts twice, for more than its money, at a price nobody checked, before its round, or by itself on terms no enabled template covers | Shares issued for nothing, or dilution nobody approved | High |

## 4. What remains

- The Board's whole cap table "if granted" arrives with the option pool's fully diluted cap table (slice 5a-ii); a grant's
  exercise, forfeiture and expiry with its events (slice 5b).
- The `fyniq.read.equity` scope arrives with `p1-mcp-first-party`. Until then, no AI tool reads equity data.
