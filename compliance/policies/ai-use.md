# AI use policy

## Purpose

This policy says how the company uses AI, in Fyniq and outside it, so that a person stays accountable for every
decision AI helps with. It follows IMDA's model frameworks for AI governance, including the one for AI agents:
bound what AI can do before it is used, and keep a named person accountable for what it does.

## Scope

Fyniq's AI assistant and agents, and any other AI tool used for company work.

## What we do

- **AI proposes; people decide.** AI may read and suggest. A change it proposes takes effect only when a named person
  approves it.
- **Bounded.** Each AI task does only what it is approved for; anything else is off until an Owner turns it on.
- **Data.** We do not give AI tools personal data, NRIC numbers, bank details or secrets unless the task needs them and
  the tool is approved for them.
- **Checked.** Figures come from the books, not from the AI's memory; we check what AI tells us before we rely on it.
- **Honest.** AI content is marked as AI wherever it appears.

## What Fyniq enforces

- The AI holds no write access to the books (CTL-AI-01); every change it proposes is approved by a person, and that
  approval is reconciled each night (CTL-AI-02); automatic approval of AI proposals cannot be configured (CTL-AI-03).
- Sensitive data is kept from the AI unless enabled (CTL-AI-06), and AI clients connected over MCP can only propose
  (CTL-MCP-01).

## Roles

The Owner decides which AI tools and tasks the company uses, and reviews this policy at least once a year and whenever
the company starts using a new kind of AI.
