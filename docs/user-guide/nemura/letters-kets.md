# Record a person's key employment terms and issue their KETs

## What it is

The Employment Act asks the employer to give an employee the key employment terms (KETs) in writing, no later than 14 days after the employment starts, for
an employee covered by the Act whose employment lasts 14 days or more. MOM's guidelines list 17 items, from the employer's and the employee's names to the notice
period. Nemura keeps the list in its country pack and fills it from the person's record and from the terms you record here. Items that do not apply, such as the
duration of a contract that is not for a fixed term, say so in words.

## Before you start

- The letterhead is set, the **Key employment terms** type is switched on and its template is published. See [Set up the letterhead, the letter types and the templates](letters-templates.md).
- The person's record, their working pattern and their legal classification are recorded: the letter reads the working days and hours, and whether overtime applies,
  from them.

## Steps

1. Open the person under **People**. Under **Key employment terms**, select **Record the terms**.
2. The people team writes the main duties, the notice period, the other medical benefits and, for a fixed-term contract, its last day.
3. The Payroll administrator or the Owner writes the pay: the salary period, the basic salary, the fixed allowances and deductions, the overtime payment period if
   it is not the salary period, and other pay such as bonuses. The people team does not see or write the pay.
4. Select **Ask for the KETs letter**. If a fact is missing, Nemura names it, such as the pay terms; record it and ask again.
5. Another member of the people team checks the letter and the Owner signs it. See [Check and sign letters](letters-issue.md).

## What happens next

- New terms start on a day you choose and end the earlier ones the day before. A record of terms is history and never changes.
- **Checks** shows a person who started 14 or more days ago with no issued KETs, and a reminder in the 3 days before.
- The audit trail says that pay terms were set, never what they were.

## Common problems

- **Nemura says the pay terms are not recorded.** Ask the Payroll administrator or the Owner to record them under the person's **Key employment terms**.
- **The new terms are refused as starting too early.** Choose a day after the current terms began.
- **The overtime item says it does not apply.** Overtime pay under the Employment Act applies to a workman, or a non-workman, up to the basic monthly salary
  the pack sets; the person's classification and salary decide. Check the classification if it looks wrong.
