# Build and publish the monthly board pack

## Find it

In Expert mode, open **Reports** and choose **Board pack**. An Owner, Controller or Accountant builds it; an
Owner or Controller publishes it.

## Start a month's pack

**Start the pack** makes a draft for the month. A month has one draft at a time. Its figures, the profit
and loss, the balance sheet, the cash flow and the KPIs of the month, are read as they stand now, so you
see what would be published.

## Write the texts

The **highlights**, the **commentary** and the **asks of the board** are written by a person. The pack
never needs the AI: if the AI drafts commentary, you accept it into the text and edit it, and the text is
yours. **Save the texts** keeps them; each save is the next edit.

## Publish it

**Publish** reads the figures at the ledger's revision at that moment and freezes them with the texts. The
pack a board read is the pack it keeps reading: a posting made afterwards does not change it. A month that
is not closed is published all the same, and the pack says so.

A published pack never changes. To correct it, start the pack of the same month again: that is the next
version, and it supersedes the first, which stays.

## Who reads it

A Board viewer reads the published packs on the **Board and investor portal** after signing in. They do not
see drafts. There is no link that opens a pack without signing in.
