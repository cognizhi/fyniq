# Send an investor update

## Find it

- In Expert mode, **Equity** has **Investor updates**. An Owner or Controller makes and sends an update.

(src: docs/user-guide/investor-updates.md#find-it)

## Build it from a published pack

- An update is built from a published board pack: its KPIs, cash, runway and statements are the update's figures. It is monthly or quarterly, with a title and its own highlights and asks.

(src: docs/user-guide/investor-updates.md#build-it-from-a-published-pack)

## Send it once

- Each recipient must be an active member who may read the board pack, such as a Board viewer. **Send it** tells each by a notification that links to the update after they sign in; it carries no figure and no file.
- The recipients are fixed when it is sent, and a sent update never changes. To say more, send a new update.

(src: docs/user-guide/investor-updates.md#send-it-once)

## Who has read it

- The list shows how many of the people it was sent to have opened it; a person's first open is recorded once. A Board viewer sees only the updates sent to them.

(src: docs/user-guide/investor-updates.md#who-has-read-it)
