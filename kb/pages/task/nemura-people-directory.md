# Find a colleague in Nemura's people directory

## What it is

- The **People** page lists everyone who works here today with name, job title, department, manager and work email; everyone who signs in can read it.
- It never shows anything restricted: no identity number, bank account, pay or date of birth. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/people.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Open **People** from the top of any page; sign in first ([[nemura.sign-in]]).
2. Type a name, job title, department or manager under **Find a person**.
3. The people team (HR, the Payroll administrator and the Owner) can select a name to open the record ([[nemura.person]]).

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/people.md#steps)

## Add a person

- The HR officer and the HR administrator see **Add a person** on the People page: legal name, start date, job title and kind of employment are required; department, line manager, preferred name, work email and phone are optional.
- The job starts on the start date, and sending the form twice is refused, not added again. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/people.md#add-a-person)

## When it looks wrong

- Nobody listed: the HR team adds people.
- A colleague missing: someone who has not started or has left is not listed.
- A name that is not a link: only the people team opens a record. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/people.md#common-problems)
