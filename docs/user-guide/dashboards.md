# Arrange your own dashboard

## Find it

Open **Reports** and choose **Dashboard**. It shows your tiles: the KPIs you watch.

## Which arrangement you see

You see your own arrangement if you saved one. If not, you see the arrangement an Owner or Administrator set
for your role. If there is none, you see the default: cash, runway, net burn, revenue, gross margin and the
year's net profit.

## Arrange it

**Add a tile** from the list of KPIs. Move a tile earlier or later, choose its width (a third, half or the
whole row), or remove it. **Save my dashboard** keeps it as yours. **Use the default** clears yours, so the
role's arrangement, or the default, shows again.

Each tile is placed once. A dashboard holds up to 24 tiles.

## What a tile shows

A tile shows the KPI as the server worked it out, the month before beside it, and when it was read. Select
a tile to see what is behind it. A tile shows only what **you** may read: a layout never gives anyone
figures their roles do not read.

## Alerts about spend

When a closed month's spend in an expense category is well above its trend, the people who approve budgets
are told. The trend is the mean of the months before it (at least six); the alert needs the spend to be
more than three standard deviations above the mean and at least a tenth above it. Each category and month
is alerted once.
