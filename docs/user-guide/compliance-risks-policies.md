# Keep the risk register

## What it is

**Risks and policies** (from **Compliance**, Expert mode) holds the company's risk register: each risk with the role that
answers for it, how likely it is and how much it would hurt, how it is reduced, and when it is next reviewed. Each risk
has a rating, high, medium or low, from its likelihood and its impact; the highest come first.

The Owner, the Administrator, the Controller, the Accountant, the Auditor and the Compliance officer see it. The
Compliance officer adds, changes and reviews risks.

A company that runs Fyniq for itself starts with the risks of building and running Fyniq already in its register; every
other company starts with an empty one.

Below the register is the policy library: the company's policies, which every member reads and acknowledges, and which
the Owner and the Compliance officer publish.

## Add or change a risk

1. Select **Add a risk**, or **Change** beside a risk.
2. Say the risk in a sentence, choose its **Owner**, its **Likelihood** and **Impact**, and say how it is reduced under
   **Mitigation**.
3. Choose how often it is reviewed: every 3 months is each quarter. A new risk's first review is the end of the next
   quarter unless you choose a day.
4. Select **Add the risk** or **Save the change**.

A change saves the risk's next version; the earlier versions are kept, and each is in the audit trail. A risk that no
longer applies is closed, not removed: choose **Closed** under **State**.

## Review a risk

When a risk falls due, select **Review** beside it, add a note if you want, and select **Record the review**. Its next
review falls its review interval after today. A review that is overdue is called out at the top of the page.
