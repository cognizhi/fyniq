# Change how Fyniq looks

## What it is

You can choose Fyniq's theme, a high-contrast version of it, how dense tables are, and whether
things move on screen. The choices change only how Fyniq looks for you, never your data.

## Before you start

- For now your choices are kept in this browser. On another computer or browser, choose them again.

## Steps

Select **Appearance** in the header, or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type the choice,
such as "dark theme".

### Theme

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System follows your device's light or dark setting and
changes when it does.

### High contrast

Choose **High contrast** for stronger text and borders, **Standard** for the usual look, or
**System** to follow your device's contrast setting. High contrast works with both themes.

### Density

Choose **Comfortable** (rows 40 px high) or **Compact** (rows 32 px high, to see more at once).
Density is an Expert choice: Basic mode always uses Comfortable.

### Motion

Choose **Reduce motion** to show every change at once, without animation, or **Motion on** for
short transitions. If your device asks for reduced motion, Fyniq always follows it. An
Administrator can also switch motion off for the whole organisation.

## Common problems

- **The theme changed by itself.** You chose **System**, and your device switched between light
  and dark. Choose **Light** or **Dark** to keep one.
- **Windows high-contrast themes.** When Windows uses a contrast theme, its colours win over
  Fyniq's, and the focus outline stays visible.
