# Use your Nemura account after you leave

## What it is

- After a person's last day the same sign-in opens **My documents**, a narrow account with their own documents and letters and a way to ask the people team for a copy of one they lost.
- It holds no role, whatever the person held before, and nothing else in Nemura is open. It ends 12 months after the last day, the period the company keeps a former employee's records. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leaver-account.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Sign in as before, with the company email through the provider or with a passkey; see [[nemura.sign-in]].
2. Open a document or a letter; a letter can be shared with a link that works for seven days.
3. To ask for a copy of a lost document, write which one under **Ask for a copy of a lost document** and select **Send the question**.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leaver-account.md#steps)

## What happens next

- The people team answers within 3 working days; for a letter or the key employment terms they ask for it again as a new copy (see [[nemura.letters.kets]]), which shows once it is signed.
- When the account ends the person can no longer sign in. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leaver-account.md#what-happens-next)

## When it looks wrong

- "That is not part of your account now": after the last day only documents, letters and a question to the people team are open.
- A wrong last day: ask the people team to correct it; the account follows it.
- Cannot sign in: the 12 months have passed. See also [[nemura.ask-hr]]. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leaver-account.md#common-problems)
