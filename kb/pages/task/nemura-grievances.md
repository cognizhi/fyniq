# Raise a concern, and handle one

## What it is

- Anyone who works here can raise a concern in confidence. Only one HR administrator or the Owner reads it; the people it is about never can, whatever their role, and nobody else can. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/grievances.md#what-it-is)

## Raising one

1. Open Raise a concern, write what happened, when and who was there in 20 to 4000 characters.
2. Choose the people it is about (up to five), or leave it empty for a practice.
3. Select Send in confidence.

- You are told who looks into it; if everyone who could is involved it waits and shows as taken later. You cannot handle your own. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/grievances.md#raise-a-concern)

## Reading the outcome

- Under Your concerns you see waiting, being looked into or decided, and once decided the written outcome and its conclusion: substantiated, partly substantiated, not substantiated or no finding. You do not see the inquiry's notes. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/grievances.md#read-the-outcome)

## Handling one

- Only an HR administrator or the Owner who is not involved handles a grievance: they read it (recorded), add notes, interviews and findings that are never changed, write the outcome once, or pass it on with a reason to the next eligible person. One whose handler has left waits in Waiting to be taken, where an HR administrator or the Owner who is not involved may read and take it. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/grievances.md#handle-a-grievance)

## How long it is kept

- A decided grievance and its inquiry are kept five years after the outcome, then listed for the Owner's review and deleted only after it, with a proof and never its words; a legal hold keeps it. The five years is the product's choice [owner to confirm]. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/grievances.md#how-long-a-grievance-is-kept)

## When it looks wrong

- "No such grievance" means you may not read it, and nobody is told why. "Nobody else can handle it" means everyone else raised it or is involved. An outcome is written once. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/grievances.md#common-problems)
