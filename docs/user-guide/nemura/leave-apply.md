# Apply for leave

## What it is

In Nemura you ask for leave from **My leave** or the **Apply for leave** tile on your home. The
form shows your balance after the request and who else in your team is away, so you can decide
before you send it.

## Before you start

- Know the days you want. Half days are fine: choose the morning or the afternoon.
- If your leave needs a document, such as a medical certificate, have a photo or the file ready.

## Steps

1. Open **Apply for leave**.
2. Under **What kind of leave?**, choose the type.
3. Under **When?**, choose the first and last day, and **Full day**, **Morning only** or
   **Afternoon only** where you need a half day.
4. Check the balance after this request. For types counted in working days, public holidays
   and your rest days are not counted.
5. Look at who else in your team is away. Nemura warns you about a clash, but you can still send
   the request.
6. Add a note or a document under **Anything to add?** if you want to.
7. Select **Send to** your manager (the button shows their name).

## What happens next

- Your line manager approves or refuses it. A refusal always comes with a reason.
- HR is told, and for some types, such as long unpaid leave, HR approves as well.
- The team calendar shows you as **Away**; only people with the right see the leave type.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The request is more than your balance | Nemura refuses it, unless the leave type allows a negative balance. Shorten it, or ask HR about another type |
| Someone in your team is away on the same days | It is a warning only. Mention it in your note if it helps your manager |
