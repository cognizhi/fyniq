# Claim back money you spent for the company

## What it is

**My claims** is where you ask the company to pay back what you spent for it: a receipt, or a
trip in your own car. It also shows what the company still owes you, the lines of a company card
you hold, and any cash advance you were given. If claims are all you do in Fyniq, Fyniq opens on
My claims and shows only your own items; you never see the company's figures.

## Before you start

- Have the receipt ready: a photo, a screenshot or a PDF.
- Know the company's expense policy: each category can have a limit per line, a receipt it needs
  above an amount, and who attended for a meal above an amount. Claims are due within the claim
  deadline the company sets.
- Using Nemura? Open **Claims** on your Nemura home. It opens My claims with the same sign-in,
  and **Back to People** takes you back.

## Claim a receipt

1. Open **My claims** and select **New claim**.
2. Say what the claim is for, and its date.
3. For each receipt: who you paid, the date on the receipt, the category, the amount and its
   currency, and the GST code (TX when the receipt shows GST, NR when the merchant is not
   GST-registered). Add the GST the receipt shows, if any.
4. For a receipt in another currency, Fyniq converts it at the day's rate. If your card statement
   shows what left your account in Singapore dollars, enter that instead: it is what the company
   pays you back.
5. For a meal or entertainment, name who attended.
6. Select **Save draft**. You can come back and change a draft as often as you like.

## Attach the receipt

1. On the saved claim, under **Evidence**, choose the file and say what it is, such as a receipt.
2. Select **Attach**. Fyniq scans the file and strips image details such as location.
3. On the receipt's line, pick the file under **Its receipt**.

Above the category's receipt amount, a line cannot be submitted without its receipt. A file that
is already on another record blocks the line until you say why it is not a duplicate.

## Submit a claim

1. Fix anything marked **Fix**. Lines marked **Review** can still be sent: the approver sees why.
2. Select **Submit** and confirm it's you.
3. Another person approves it. When a line is above its category's limit or past the claim
   deadline, finance approves it too; a late claim says why it is late.
4. Changed your mind? Select **Withdraw**: the claim becomes a draft again.

Nobody approves their own claim.

## Follow your claims

- Each claim shows its stage: draft, submitted, returned, approved or paid.
- **What the company owes you** is the total of your approved claims not yet paid back.
- A returned claim shows the approver's comment at the top of My claims: open it, fix it and
  submit it again. Each version is kept.

## Complete your company-card lines

When you hold a company card, its payments show under **Card lines** once finance imports the
card's statement.

1. Select **Complete** on a payment.
2. Say what it was for: the category and the GST code of its receipt. If it was for yourself,
   choose personal spend instead.
3. Attach its receipt.

Personal spend becomes an amount you owe the company. It shows under **Cash advances** until you
refund it, and no claim settles it.

## Cash advances

When finance gives you money before a trip, it shows under **Cash advances** with what is still
open. Choose it under **Settles a cash advance** on your claims for the trip: the claims settle
what is open, and anything above it is paid back to you. Refund what is left by bank transfer;
finance records it. An advance still open after the claim deadline is flagged for review.

## What happens next

- Once approved, the claim is paid back by bank transfer, and later in the next payment batch.
  A reimbursement of what you actually spent for work is not part of your pay.
- A claim in another currency is paid back in Singapore dollars.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The receipt was already claimed or captured | The line is blocked until you say why it is not a duplicate; the approver sees your reason |
| A line is above the category's limit | It can still be sent; finance approves it too |
| The claim is past the deadline | Say why it is late; finance approves it too |
| The claim came back as returned | Read the approver's note, fix the claim and submit it again |
| A card payment does not show | Finance has not imported the card's statement yet, or the card is not set up with you as its holder |
