# Apply for leave

## What it is

In Nemura you ask for annual leave from **Apply for leave**, which you reach from **My leave**. Before you send it, the form
shows what the request costs day by day, your balance after it and who else in your team is away.

## Before you start

- Annual leave has to be granted for the year first: see [See your leave balance](my-leave.md).
- The people team must have set your working pattern, so your days can be counted. If it has not, the form says so.
- Know the first and last day. A half day is allowed on the first day, the last day or both.

## Steps

1. Open **Apply for leave**.
2. Choose the **First day** and the **Last day**. Leave the last day empty for one day.
3. Tick **Only half of the first day** or **Only half of the last day** where you need a half day.
4. Read **How the days were counted**: public holidays and your rest days cost nothing. A holiday that falls on your rest
   day moves to your next working day. Check the balance after this request.
5. Look at **Away in your team on those days**. It shows names and dates, never the type of leave. It is for information:
   you can still send the request.
6. Add a note if you want to, then select **Send to my manager**.

## What happens next

- Your line manager approves or refuses it. A refusal always comes with a reason, and the days come back to your balance.
- The days are reserved the moment you send it, so two requests cannot both take your last days.
- Once the leave has been taken, the people team records it as taken.
- You can cancel a request that is still waiting, or an approved one before it starts. Leave that has started is
  corrected by the people team.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The request is more than your balance | Nemura refuses it and says how many days you have left. Shorten it, or ask the people team about your balance |
| You already have leave on some of those days | Cancel the first request, or choose other days |
| The request runs over the new year | Ask for each year on its own, so each comes out of its own balance |
| The public holidays for that year are not in the country pack yet | Ask again once they are published |
| Those dates have no working days | They are rest days or public holidays: there is nothing to ask for |
| No working pattern is set | Ask the people team to set it |
