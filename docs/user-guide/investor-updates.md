# Send an investor update

## Find it

In Expert mode, open **Equity** and choose **Investor updates**. An Owner or Controller makes and sends an
update.

## Build it from a published pack

An update is built from a **published** board pack: its KPIs, cash, runway and statements are the update's
figures. Give it a title and choose **monthly** or **quarterly**. Then write the update's own
**highlights** and **asks**.

## Send it once

Choose the members to send it to: each must be an active member who may read the board pack, such as a
Board viewer. **Send it** tells each of them by a notification that links to the update after they sign in.
The notification carries **no figure and no file**.

The recipients are fixed when it is sent, and a sent update never changes. To say more, send a new update.

## Who has read it

The list shows how many of the people it was sent to have opened it. A person's first open is recorded once.
A Board viewer sees only the updates sent to them.
