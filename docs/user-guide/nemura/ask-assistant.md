# Ask the assistant about your leave, pay day and payslip

## What it is

**Ask the assistant** answers questions about your own leave balance, your pay day, what a rule of the Singapore pack says and what a line of your payslip is. Each answer says where it comes from: your own record, or the pack version and the official page it was read from. When a rule has been read from an official page but not yet verified a second time, the answer says so and tells you to confirm it with HR.

## Before you start

- The assistant must be turned on: the Owner does that in the assistant's settings. If it is off, the page says so.
- Your login must be linked to your person record.

## Steps

1. Open **Ask the assistant** from your home page.
2. Write your question, such as "How much leave do I have left?", "When is my pay day?" or "What is the CPF line on my payslip?", and select **Ask**.
3. Read the answer and its sources. An answer is marked as an AI answer.

## What happens next

- A payslip line is explained as a rule. Your own figures for the line are on your payslip page, beside it: no pay figure is sent to a model, and no one else's pay or records are ever read.
- The assistant never approves, changes, issues or pays anything. If you ask it to, it says so and suggests asking the people team. Your question is not kept; only the tools it used are recorded.

## Common problems

- **The assistant is off.** Ask the people team or the Owner to turn it on.
- **It could not match my question.** Ask it another way, or [ask the people team](ask-hr.md).
- **A figure looks wrong.** Every figure comes from your record in the same answer; a figure it could not check is shown as unverified. Tell the people team.
