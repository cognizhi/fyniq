# Notifications and banners

## Your notifications

- The bell in the header counts your unread notifications. Select it to open your notifications.
- A notification says what happened that concerns you: an approval waiting for you, a decision on your request,
  a security notice about your account, mail waiting in quarantine, a posting someone else made, a month closed,
  a draft made from a recurring journal or bill, bills to pay soon, or background work that failed.
- One that waits for you to act links to it, and opening it marks it read.
- Show **Unread**, **All** or **Archived**. **Mark read** and **Mark unread** change one; **Mark all read** marks
  every unread one read; **Archive** moves one to Archived for good.
- You never hear of something you did yourself.

## Choose how they reach you

- Open **Settings → Notifications**. Every notification always shows in the app; this page decides how each kind
  reaches you by email.
- For each kind choose **Immediately**, **Daily digest**, **Weekly digest** or **Not by email**.
- Recommended: approvals and security notices at once, everything else in the daily digest at 08:00 Singapore
  time. A kind you changed shows **Changed from recommended**, with **Reset to recommended**.
- Security notices are **Always on**: a passkey added or removed and a change to your roles are emailed at once,
  whatever you choose. A sign-in shows here and is not emailed again, because the sign-in alert already emails
  every sign-in.
- The weekly digest goes on Mondays at 08:00.
- Email goes to the address your sign-in provider verified. Without one, notifications show in the app only.
- What an email says is your organisation's choice (**Details in notification email** in the organisation's
  settings): by default it names only the kind of notification and links to it in Fyniq.

## Quiet hours

- Turn on **Hold email during quiet hours** and set when they start and end, in Singapore time. They may run past
  midnight, such as 22:00 until 07:00.
- During quiet hours, email that would go at once waits until they end. Security notices still go at once, and
  nothing waits in the app.
- Turn the switch off to have no quiet hours.

## Send yourself a test

- **Send me a test notification** sends you a notification through every channel you receive them on: in the app,
  and by email at once unless you chose otherwise for test notifications.
- It goes the same way as any notification, so its email shows in your organisation's email delivery log.

## Banners

- Banners under the header are announcements from your Owner or Administrator, or from the people who run Fyniq,
  such as a maintenance window.
- Critical banners come first, and stay until they end. Others can be dismissed: **Dismiss** hides one for you
  on every device, not for anyone else.
- A banner's link opens a page in Fyniq, or an outside address in a new tab.
