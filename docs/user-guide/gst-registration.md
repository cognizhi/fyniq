# GST registration

## What it is

While the company is not registered for GST, **Compliance › GST** tests whether it must be. IRAS
asks a business to register when its taxable supplies are more than $1 million in a calendar year,
or are expected to be more than $1 million in the next 12 months. Until it registers, GST that
overseas suppliers charge is part of the cost.

## The two tests

- **Last calendar year**: its taxable supplies against the threshold. When they were more, apply
  between 1 Jan and 30 Jan; the registration starts on 1 Mar.
- **The forecast**: the latest forecast you recorded for the next 12 months. When it is more,
  apply within 30 days after the date of the forecast; the registration starts 2 months from it.

The page also shows this year so far, and warns once it reaches 80% of the threshold. The percentage
is a setting ([Organisation settings](organisation-settings.md)).

## Taxable supplies

Taxable supplies are the standard-rated and zero-rated sales on invoices, credit notes and bank
transactions. Exempt and out-of-scope sales, and sales of the company's own assets, are left out.

## Record a forecast

IRAS asks for supporting documents, such as signed contracts or confirmed orders. A business plan or
a sales target is not certain enough.

1. Select **Record a forecast**.
2. Enter the day of the forecast, the sales you expect in the 12 months from it, and what it rests on.
3. Attach the contracts or orders.
4. Select **Record**.

A later forecast replaces it.

## When a test is met

The page and **Checks** (C.1 #37) give the day to apply by and the day the registration would start.
Applying is also on the statutory calendar ([The statutory calendar](statutory-calendar.md)). Once
IRAS registers the company, record the registration in Settings › Organisation.
