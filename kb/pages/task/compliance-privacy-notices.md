# Give the privacy notices and record consent

## What it is

- Under the PDPA the company tells people what it does with their personal data. **Privacy notices and consent** (from **Compliance**, Expert mode) holds the **members' notice**, for the people who use Fyniq, and the **contacts' notice**, for the people at customers and suppliers, with consent for any purpose that needs it.
- The Owner, the Auditor and the Compliance officer see it; the Owner and the Compliance officer publish the notices ([[members.roles]]).

(src: docs/user-guide/compliance-privacy-notices.md#what-it-is)

## Publish a notice

- Each notice starts as a draft from the PDPC's guides; clauses marked [counsel to confirm] bear on the law, so have counsel confirm them first.
- Select **Draft**, adopt or edit and **Save the draft**, then **Publish version**; a published version never changes.
- Members are then asked to read and accept the members' notice under **Acknowledge policy** on **Home** and in the **Inbox**, a new member first ([[compliance.policies]]).
- Every email to a customer or supplier ends with the contacts' notice's first paragraph and the Data Protection Officer's contact ([[settings.organisation]]); contacts do not acknowledge it.

(src: docs/user-guide/compliance-privacy-notices.md#publish-a-notice)

## Consent

- The work, the business relationship and the records the law requires need no consent [counsel to confirm]; no purpose is defined at first.
- For something more, such as a newsletter, the Compliance officer adds a purpose under **Purposes and consent**, saying whose data and whether it needs consent; a purpose never changes.
- **Record** notes a consent given or withdrawn, and how; a consent is given only when none stands and withdrawn only when one does, and withdrawal stops further use. Every record stays, in the list and the audit trail ([[compliance.audit-trail]]).

(src: docs/user-guide/compliance-privacy-notices.md#consent)
