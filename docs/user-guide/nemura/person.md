# Read a person's record and their job history

## What it is

A person's record holds their name, contact, start date and any leaving date, their job history and, for the
people team, their legal classification. A job change never edits the old record: it ends it the day the new one
starts, so you can see who held which job, under whom, on any day.

## Before you start

- You may read a record if you are in the people team, if it is yours, or if the person reports to you.

## Steps

1. Open **People** and select the person's name.
2. Read **Contact**, then **Job history**: each row shows from when to when, the job title, department, employment type and worksite.
3. The people team also sees **Legal classification**: contract of service or for service, Employment Act cover, Part IV class, residency and date of birth. A contractor (a contract for service) is not covered by the Employment Act.

4. The HR officer, the HR administrator, the Payroll administrator and the Owner also see **Key employment terms**: the terms the Employment Act asks the employer to give in writing. Only the Payroll administrator and the Owner see and write the pay. See [Record a person's key employment terms and issue their KETs](letters-kets.md).

## Common problems

- **There is no such person, or you may not see their record.** Nemura does not say which: ask the people team.
- **The classification is missing.** It has not been set, or you are not in the people team.
