# Find evidence, and what is missing

## What it is

**Evidence** (from **Accounting**, Expert mode) lists the files attached across the company's
records: bills, invoices, journals, bank transactions, expense claims, changes of bank details,
founders' money and Owner successions. You see only the files on the records your role lets you see.

## Find evidence

1. Choose the **Records** to look in, or leave **All you may see**.
2. Type part of the **Evidence type**, such as "invoice" or "resolution", if you know it.
3. Set **Records from** and **to** to look at the records of a period, such as a month.
4. Select **Find**.

- The newest records come first. Each file shows its record, the record's date, the file's name,
  what it is and when it was attached; a record's number opens it.
- A file whose text shows an NRIC or FIN in full is marked **Shows an NRIC**.

## Missing evidence

- **Missing evidence** lists the posted records of the open months that have no file while their
  evidence is required or recommended: a bill without the supplier's invoice or receipt, or a
  manual journal above the evidence threshold without its supporting calculation or document.
- Open the record and attach the file in its **Evidence** panel; it leaves the list.
- The same list is the check "Posted records have their evidence" on **Checks**.
- What each kind of record needs is set in [Organisation settings](organisation-settings.md#evidence-rules).
