# Record and approve the accounting framework and policies

## What it is

Your statements are prepared under an accounting framework, such as SFRS for Small Entities, in a
particular edition. Your accounting policies say how Fyniq treats things such as revenue, government
grants, leases and untaken leave. Both are kept under **Settings → Accounting framework and policies**
(Expert mode), one financial year at a time.

Each framework or policy you record is a version. It applies from the first day of a financial year
until a later version replaces it, so a policy recorded for this year carries into the next one. A
version never changes: to correct one, record a new version.

Fyniq never writes a policy for you. Each policy says what it decides; the words are your
accountant's.

## Record the framework

1. Open **Settings → Accounting framework and policies** and choose **This financial year** or
   **Next financial year**.
2. Select **Record the framework**.
3. Choose the framework. Fyniq recommends SFRS for Small Entities if your accountant confirms the
   company qualifies, and fills in the edition that applies to the year.
4. Select **Check** to see that it can be recorded, then **Record the framework**.

SFRS for Small Entities has a Third Edition for financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2027.
A year from then needs the framework recorded with that edition, even if an earlier year's framework
carries into it.

## Write a policy

1. Under **Accounting policies**, select **Write** beside the policy (or **Change** to replace it).
2. Write the policy as your accountant decided it. Where it does not apply to the company, say so,
   for example "The company has no leases."
3. Select **Check**, then **Record the policy**.

The capitalisation threshold policy approves the amount set under **Settings → Organisation →
Defaults**; change the amount there.

## Confirm and approve

A member who holds the Accountant role confirms the framework (that the company may use it) and
approves each policy. Nobody signs a version they recorded themselves: if you wrote it, another member
with the Accountant role signs it. Each version is signed once, with who signed it and when.

1. Open the year.
2. Select **Confirm** beside the framework, or **Approve** beside a policy.

The Owner and the Controller can record the framework and policies but cannot confirm or approve
them.

## Management statements

Until the framework in force for a year is confirmed and every policy in force is approved, that
year's statements are titled **Management statements** and name no framework. The page lists what is
still to do. Once everything is signed, they are titled **Financial statements** and say what they are
prepared under, for example "Prepared under SFRS for Small Entities (Third Edition)".

A new version of a policy waits for approval again, and so the year goes back to management
statements until it is approved.

## Common problems

- **You wrote it, so another accountant signs it**: ask another member who holds the Accountant role.
- **Signed already, or replaced**: the version is signed already, or a later version from the same
  year replaces it; sign the latest version instead.
- **Not the first day of a financial year**: a version applies from the start of a financial year. If
  the years are not set yet, set the books start and the first year end under **Settings →
  Organisation**.
