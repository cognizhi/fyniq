# Tell us you're sick

## What it is

Sick leave is not applied for: you tell your manager and HR that you are away, with **I'm sick**
in **My leave**. Nobody has to approve it. HR checks the medical certificate, and only HR sees it.

## Before you start

- Outpatient sick leave and hospitalisation leave share one limit: the outpatient days count
  towards the hospitalisation days.
- In Singapore, under the Employment Act, an employee gets paid sick leave after 3 months'
  service: 5 days outpatient and 15 hospitalisation at 3 months, 8 and 30 at 4 months, 11 and
  45 at 5 months, and 14 and 60 from 6 months. The 60 include the 14. Your company may give more.
- The sick leave must be certified by a registered medical practitioner.

## Steps

1. Open **My leave** and select **I'm sick**.
2. Under **When?**, give the first day and, if you know it, the last day.
3. Choose **Outpatient** or **Hospitalisation**. Nemura shows the days you have left.
4. Add the certificate with **Photo of the MC** or **e-MC file**. If you do not have it yet,
   send the notice now and add the certificate when you are back.
5. Select the button that tells your manager and HR.

## The 48-hour rule

Tell your employer, or try to, within 48 hours of being away, and give the medical certificate
when you return. **I'm sick** tells your manager and HR at once.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No sick leave balance yet | Paid sick leave starts after 3 months' service. Ask HR how your company handles the days before that |
| You have used your outpatient days | Ask HR: further days follow your company's policy |
