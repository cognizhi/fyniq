# See requests and make a flexible work request in Nemura

## What it is

- **My requests** shows where a person's leave requests and flexible work requests are.
- A **flexible work request** is a formal request to change when, where or how much a person works. Under the Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests, which took effect on 1 December 2024, the company has 2 months to respond.
- Nemura dates the request the day it is sent and shows the day the written reply is due; a clarification or a change of type does not restart that date.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/requests.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Open **My requests**: **Cancel** a leave request that is waiting, or an approved one before it starts.
2. Read each flexible work request and, once answered, its written reply.
3. To make one, fill in **Make a formal flexible work request** (what to change, start, end if any, why) and select **Send the request**.
4. **Withdraw** it while it waits for a reply.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/requests.md#steps)

## Hours worked on special days

- A Part IV employee enters overtime, work on a rest day and work on a public holiday under their requests, one day at a time, without clocking; for rest-day work they say whether it was at the employer's request or theirs.
- The manager or the people team approves each day and a pay run uses it once. Overtime beyond the monthly limit in the country pack stays recorded and is paid, and the people team is told; a person who is not a Part IV employee on that day is refused.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/requests.md#hours-worked-on-special-days)

## What happens next

- The HR officer, the HR administrator or the Owner answers in writing: accepted, refused, or an alternative; a refusal or an alternative gives its reason, and nobody answers their own request ([[leave.approve]]).
- A request with no written reply as the 2 months run out shows to the people team in what needs a look.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/requests.md#what-happens-next)
