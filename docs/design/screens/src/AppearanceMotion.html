Appearance and motion

Organisation defaults for how Fyniq moves and looks. Each person can still choose their own theme and reduce motion.

Change history

Interface motion

Recommended

Recommended: On. A few short transitions show where something came from and what changed; they never delay what you do. Learn more

Every change is audited with who, when and why.

Who decides

  1. The device's reduced-motion setting always winsIf a phone or computer asks for less motion, nothing moves on it, whatever is chosen here.
  2. Each person can choose Reduce motionIn Profile and security. It only removes motion, never adds it back when this is Off.
  3. This switch sets it for everyone elseOff and Reduce show final states immediately. Motion never carries meaning on its own.
