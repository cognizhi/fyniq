# The statutory calendar and what it holds

## What it is

- **Compliance › Calendar** lists every filing and payment deadline, worked out from the financial years, the GST return periods and the settings.
- Fyniq files nothing itself: the company files with IRAS, CPF Board or ACRA, then records what it did ([[compliance.record-filing]]).
- Each item says what is due, by when and why, in the words of its rule.
- Nothing due before 7 Nov 2026, the day the rules were read, is in the calendar.

(src: docs/user-guide/statutory-calendar.md#what-it-is)

## What it holds

- **GST F5**: one month after the end of each return period, while registered.
- **CPF**: by the 14th of the next month, or the next working day after a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, while the company pays staff.
- **Employment income (IR8A, AIS)**: by 1 March for the year before, while the company pays staff.
- **ECI**: within 3 months from the end of the financial year, unless waived. **Form C-S or C**: by 30 November of the year after the year the financial year ends in.
- **Annual return**: within seven months after the year's end, or eight with an overseas branch register. **AGM**: within six months, or the financial statements sent within five months so no AGM is held.
- **Applying to register for GST**, while the monitor finds a test met ([[compliance.gst]]).
- **The registers' own deadlines** (a controller to enter or lodge, share lodgements) open their record, where they are done.
- The settings **Pays staff**, **Annual general meeting** and **Keeps a branch register outside Singapore** decide what it holds ([[settings.organisation]]); a missing input shows **Not assessed**.

(src: docs/user-guide/statutory-calendar.md#what-it-holds)

## Reminders and Checks

- Those who record filings hear of each item 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before it is due, and the day after it passed with nothing recorded.
- **Checks** lists a GST return due within 7 days or past (C.2 #13) and every other deadline due within 14 days or past (C.2 #15).
- The **Inbox** shows what is due in the next 30 days.

(src: docs/user-guide/statutory-calendar.md#reminders-and-checks)
