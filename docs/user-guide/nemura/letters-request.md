# Ask for a letter

## What it is

You can ask your company for a letter about you, such as an employment confirmation for a bank, a landlord or a visa. The letter
is filled from your record and shows only facts of it: your name, job title and start date, never your salary unless the letter
type is for that and you ask for it. The people team checks it and the signatory signs it.

## Before you start

- The company must issue that type of letter and have a template for it. If **Ask for a letter** says the company issues no
  letters yet, ask the people team.
- Your record must hold what the letter shows. If a fact is missing, Nemura names it and the people team records it first.

## Steps

1. Open **Ask for a letter**.
2. Choose **Which letter?** The sentence under it says what the letter says.
3. Under **What is it for?**, write the purpose, such as "a bank loan".
4. Select **Send the request**. Nemura shows the day it is due.
5. Follow it under **My requests**. When it is issued, select **Download**, or **Make a link that works for seven days** to share
   it. The link stops working after seven days and delivers that one letter only.

## What happens next

- An HR officer checks the letter. That draws the exact PDF and gives it a reference. Nobody checks their own letter.
- The signatory reads that PDF and signs it with a passkey. The exact bytes are kept as evidence, and changing one byte breaks
  the match.
- You can **Withdraw** a request that is not issued yet. A refusal always comes with a reason.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The company does not issue that letter yet | Ask the people team to switch the type on |
| The template has no published version yet | The HR administrator publishes one first |
| The letter needs a value your record does not hold | The people team records it, then you ask again |
| The local build cannot draw a character | Local mode draws Latin letters only; the platform's service draws other scripts |
