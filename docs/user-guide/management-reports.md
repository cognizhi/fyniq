# Read the management reports

## Find them

In Expert mode, **Reports** lists **Sales by customer and item**, **Spend by supplier** and **Top
expenses**. Each opens the management reports, with a tab per report. **Who owes us** and **Whom we
owe** are tabs too; they open the customers' statements and the bills to pay.

Choose the period: this month, last month, the year to date, this or last financial year, or any
run of months. Each row shows its amount in the base currency and its share of the total, largest
first.

## Sales by customer and by item

**Sales by customer** is what each customer was invoiced in the months, less the credit notes, net
of GST. **Sales by item** is the same by item, with the quantity sold less the quantity credited;
lines without an item are one row. Revenue spread over months counts in full in the month it is
invoiced.

A voided or edited invoice counts in the month it was changed, as in the profit and loss. Money
received at the bank without an invoice is in the profit and loss, not here.

## Spend by supplier

**Spend by supplier** is what each supplier billed in the months, less their credit notes, net of the
GST the company can claim. GST it cannot claim, such as when it is not GST-registered, is part of
the cost. Money paid at the bank without a bill, and expense claims, are in the profit and loss
and the top expenses, not here.

## Top expenses

**Top expenses** are the ten largest expense accounts in the profit and loss for the months, and the
rest as one line. Select an account to see its lines in the general ledger.

## Who sees them

Anyone who can read every account sees all four. Someone whose view of the reports is limited to
sales, such as an AR specialist, sees the sales reports only; someone limited to purchases, such
as an AP specialist, sees the spend by supplier only.

Every figure is read at one ledger revision, which the report shows, so the same months read again
at that revision give the same figures.
