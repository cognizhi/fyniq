# Report sick leave in Nemura

## How sick leave works

- Sick leave is not applied for: **I'm sick** in **My leave** tells the manager and HR, and
  nobody approves it. HR checks the medical certificate, and only HR sees it.
  (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leave-sick.md#what-it-is)
- Outpatient and hospitalisation leave share one limit: outpatient days count towards the
  hospitalisation days. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leave-sick.md#before-you-start)

## Singapore's sick-leave days

- Under the Employment Act, paid sick leave starts after 3 months' service: 5 days outpatient
  and 15 hospitalisation at 3 months, 8 and 30 at 4 months, 11 and 45 at 5 months, and 14 and 60
  from 6 months; the 60 include the 14. A company may give more.
- It must be certified by a registered medical practitioner.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leave-sick.md#before-you-start)

## Steps

1. **My leave** → **I'm sick**; give the first day and, if known, the last.
2. Choose **Outpatient** or **Hospitalisation**; Nemura shows the days left.
3. Add the certificate (**Photo of the MC** or **e-MC file**), or send now and add it on return.
4. Send: the manager and HR are told at once.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leave-sick.md#steps)

## The 48-hour rule

- Tell the employer, or try to, within 48 hours of being away, and give the certificate on
  return. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leave-sick.md#the-48-hour-rule)

## Common problems

- **No balance yet**: paid sick leave starts after 3 months' service; HR explains the company's
  approach before that.
- **Outpatient days used up**: further days follow the company's policy; ask HR.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/leave-sick.md#common-problems)
