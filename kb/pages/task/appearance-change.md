# Change how Fyniq looks: theme, high contrast, density and motion

## What the choices do

- A person chooses the theme, high contrast, table density and motion for themselves.
- The choices change only how Fyniq looks, never the data.
- For now they are kept in the browser, so another computer or browser needs them chosen again.
- Open them from **Appearance** in the header, or from search and commands (**Ctrl+K**, **⌘K** on a Mac) by typing the choice, such as "dark theme": [[home.overview]].

(src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#what-it-is) (src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#before-you-start) (src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#steps)

## Theme and high contrast

- **Light**, **Dark** or **System**; System follows the device's light or dark setting and changes with it.
- **High contrast** gives stronger text and borders, **Standard** the usual look, **System** follows the device; it works with both themes.

(src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#theme) (src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#high-contrast)

## Density and motion

- **Comfortable** rows are 40 px high; **Compact** rows are 32 px high.
- Density is an Expert choice: Basic mode always uses Comfortable.
- **Reduce motion** shows every change at once; **Motion on** keeps short transitions.
- A device that asks for reduced motion always wins, and an Administrator can switch motion off for the whole organisation.

(src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#density) (src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#motion)

## Common problems

- The theme changed by itself: **System** is chosen and the device switched; choose **Light** or **Dark** to keep one.
- With a Windows contrast theme, its colours win over Fyniq's and the focus outline stays visible.

(src: docs/user-guide/appearance.md#common-problems)
