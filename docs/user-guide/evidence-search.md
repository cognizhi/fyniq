# Find evidence, and what is missing

## What it is

**Evidence** (from **Accounting**, Expert mode) lists the files attached across the company's
records: bills, invoices, journals, bank transactions, expense claims, changes of bank details,
founders' money, Owner successions, the equity register, statutory filings and payments, and GST
forecasts. You see only the files on the records your role lets you see.

## Find evidence

1. Choose the **Records** to look in, or leave **All you may see**.
2. Type part of the **Evidence type**, such as "invoice" or "resolution", if you know it.
3. Set **Records from** and **to** to look at the records of a period, such as a month.
4. Tick **Only files I attached** to see only the files you added yourself.
5. Select **Find**.

- The newest records come first. Each file shows its record, the record's date, the file's name,
  what it is and when it was attached; a record's number opens it, or the page that lists it.
- A file whose text shows an NRIC or FIN in full is marked **Shows an NRIC**.
- The first 50 files show; select **Show more** for the next ones.

## Open a file

In a record's **Evidence** panel, and in each row of **Find evidence**, each file has a button to
open it:

- **Show** (the eye) shows a picture, such as a photo of a receipt or a screenshot, below the list.
  Select **Hide** to close it. A PDF is saved rather than shown.
- **Save** (the arrow) saves any other file, such as a PDF or a spreadsheet, to your device. Fyniq
  keeps only the letters, digits, spaces, dots, dashes and underscores of its name for the saved copy.

You open a file only on a record your role lets you see; an Employee opens the receipts of their own
claims. Fyniq checks the file against the fingerprint recorded when it was attached before it sends
it, and records each opening in the audit trail. A file taken off a draft cannot be opened.

## How long files are kept

Once a record is posted, its files are kept in a locked store that nobody can delete from, not even
an Owner:

- until the end of the financial year in which the record's transactions complete, plus the records'
  retention (7 years unless your organisation keeps them longer);
- for a contract on an invoice or a bill whose revenue or cost is spread over months, the last month
  of the spread is when its transactions complete: a contract for 2027 to 2029 is kept until the end
  of the financial year that holds the spread's last month, plus the retention;
- if the record changes so that its transactions complete later, such as a spread lengthened by an
  approved edit, Fyniq keeps its files longer from that night. A file is never kept for less time
  than it was.

## Missing evidence

- **Missing evidence** lists the posted records of the open months that have no file while their
  evidence is required or recommended: a bill without the supplier's invoice or receipt, a
  manual journal above the evidence threshold without its supporting calculation or document, or a
  filing recorded as filed without its acknowledgement.
- A filing's line opens the statutory calendar, where you withdraw the record and record it again
  with the file.
- Open the record and attach the file in its **Evidence** panel; it leaves the list.
- The same list is the check "Posted records have their evidence" on **Checks**.
- What each kind of record needs is set in [Organisation settings](organisation-settings.md#evidence-rules).
