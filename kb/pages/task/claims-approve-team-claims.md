# Approve your team's claims as a line manager

## What a line manager sees

- A line manager's reports' claims come to them first, under **To approve**, each with its
  receipt. (src: docs/user-guide/claims-approve-team.md#what-it-is)
- Being a line manager gives no other right in Fyniq: no company finances are shown.
  (src: docs/user-guide/claims-approve-team.md#what-it-is)
- The line manager comes from the people records; HR corrects a wrong one.
  (src: docs/user-guide/claims-approve-team.md#before-you-start)
- Someone who also approves bills or purchase orders for a department works in a finance
  workspace cut to that department, and the claims wait in the **Inbox**.
  (src: docs/user-guide/claims-approve-team.md#before-you-start)

## Steps

1. Open **To approve** and check the receipt, the category and, for a car trip, the purpose and
   distance.
2. Select **Approve**, or **Return with a note…** saying what to fix.
3. Approved claims go on to finance; **Recently approved by you** lists them.

(src: docs/user-guide/claims-approve-team.md#steps)

## Common problems

- **A claim from outside the team**: return it with a note and ask HR to correct the line
  manager.
- **Their own claim**: in Standard mode it cannot be approved by them; it goes to their own line
  manager.
- **"not part of your access"** on a finance link: the page is outside their rights; everything
  to approve is under To approve.

(src: docs/user-guide/claims-approve-team.md#common-problems)
