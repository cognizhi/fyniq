# GST registration and the registration monitor

## What it is

- While the company is not registered, **Compliance › GST** tests whether it must be.
- IRAS asks a business to register when its taxable supplies are more than $1 million in a calendar year, or are expected to be more than $1 million in the next 12 months.
- Until it registers, GST that overseas suppliers charge is part of the cost.

(src: docs/user-guide/gst-registration.md#what-it-is)

## The two tests

- **Last calendar year**: when its taxable supplies were more than the threshold, apply between 1 Jan and 30 Jan; the registration starts on 1 Mar.
- **The forecast**: when the latest forecast for the next 12 months is more, apply within 30 days after the date of the forecast; the registration starts 2 months from it ([[compliance.gst-forecast]]).
- The page also shows this year so far and warns once it reaches 80% of the threshold, a setting ([[settings.organisation]]).

(src: docs/user-guide/gst-registration.md#the-two-tests)

## Taxable supplies

- The standard-rated and zero-rated sales on invoices, credit notes and bank transactions.
- Exempt and out-of-scope sales, and sales of the company's own assets, are left out.

(src: docs/user-guide/gst-registration.md#taxable-supplies)

## When a test is met

- The page and **Checks** (C.1 #37) give the day to apply by and the day the registration would start; applying is also on the statutory calendar ([[compliance.calendar]]).
- Once IRAS registers the company, record the registration in Settings › Organisation.

(src: docs/user-guide/gst-registration.md#when-a-test-is-met)
