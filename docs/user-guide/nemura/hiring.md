# Ask to hire, get a requisition approved and publish a job post

## What it is

A **requisition** asks to hire for a role: the role, the team, whether it is new or replaces someone, the salary range in Singapore dollars, the start date aimed for, and whether an Employment Pass or S Pass is expected. The Owner approves it. The Recruiter then writes the **job post**, which is checked for wording the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices ask employers to avoid, and records the dates the Fair Consideration Framework asks for. The candidate pipeline, interview kits and offers come in a later release.

## Before you start

- Switch **Hiring** on under **Settings, Features**.
- The Recruiter, the HR administrator and the Owner ask for requisitions and write posts. The HR officer reads them. Only the Owner approves; when you are the Owner and asked for it yourself, you may still approve it.

## Steps

1. The Recruiter opens **Hiring**, fills **Ask to hire for a role** and selects **Send the requisition**. The salary is two amounts such as 6500.00.
2. The Owner selects **Approve** or **Reject**, once.
3. For an approved or waiting requisition, the Recruiter opens **Job post**, writes the title and text and selects **Save the draft and check the wording**. Any criterion found (age, nationality, sex, marital status, pregnancy, caregiving, race, religion, language, disability, mental health) is listed. The check decides nothing: describe the skill or duty instead.
4. Once the requisition is approved, select **Publish the post**. If a flagged criterion stays, first record the genuine occupational requirement for it; the post is refused without it. A published post never changes.
5. Post the advert on MyCareersFuture yourself (there is no posting service to use) and record the day it went up under **Record the advert**. For a role that needs a pass, no offer is made until 14 consecutive days have run; the page shows the first day an offer may be made. If an exemption applies (fewer than 10 employees, an Employment Pass at S$22,500 or more a month, a role of one month or less, a transferee, or an S Pass exemption), record it with its evidence instead.
6. Select **Close the post** when the role is filled or withdrawn.
7. If the occupation, the salary, the number of vacancies or the employer's UEN changes after the advert went up, post a new advert yourself and record its day under **Record a new advert after a change**. The 14 days start again from the new day, and the old advert is kept as evidence. An advert that came down more than 3 months ago cannot be used: post a new one.

## The careers page

Under **Careers page** the Recruiter, the HR administrator or the Owner chooses an address (3 to 40 lower-case letters, digits and hyphens), the company's name and a few words, and switches the page on. Anyone can then read `/careers/<address>` with no sign-in: each published post's title, team, text and day. The page never shows a salary or a person. Switch it off to take it down; no other company can use your address.

## What happens next

- Whether the role is in the plan shows as not known yet until the link to Fyniq supplies it.
- Every step is recorded with who did it and when, never with the words of the post.

## Common problems

- **The post is refused for its wording.** It names a criterion the guidelines ask employers not to use: reword it, or record the genuine occupational requirement.
- **The check flags a word that is fine.** The list is a starting point that prompts a person to read: a flagged word such as single in single sign-on is for you to judge.
- **It says the requisition has moved on.** It was decided already, or the post was published: read the page again.
- **It says the advert came down more than 3 months ago.** An advert closed or expired for that long cannot be used for a pass hire: post a new advert and record its day.
- **The address is taken.** Another company uses it: choose a different one.
- **You cannot see Hiring.** Only the Recruiter, the HR officer, the HR administrator and the Owner do.
