# Record a GST forecast

## Steps

- IRAS asks for supporting documents, such as signed contracts or confirmed orders; a business plan or a sales target is not certain enough.
1. In **Compliance › GST** ([[compliance.gst]]), select **Record a forecast**.
2. Enter the day of the forecast, the sales you expect in the 12 months from it, and what it rests on.
3. Attach the contracts or orders, then select **Record**. A later forecast replaces it.
