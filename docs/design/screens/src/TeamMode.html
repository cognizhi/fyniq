Break-glass

Not active

For when only one founder can act, for example the other is unreachable. It lasts 24 hours, tells Arjun Rao and Grace Ho at once, shows a banner on every page, and must be reviewed afterwards. It cannot extend or renew itself.

Allowed

  • Release payments of bills, claims and pay runs already approved, to payees whose bank details have not changed in 30 days

Never allowed

  • Add or change payee bank details
  • Users, roles, team mode, approval policies or sign-in methods
  • The AI catalogue or AI settings; statutory parameters

  • Any payment to, or settlement for, the founder using it
