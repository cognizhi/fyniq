Record a supplier's bill so Fyniq knows what you owe, when it is due, and which GST you can claim.

  1. 1Go to Purchases › Bills and choose New bill, or forward the PDF to bills@cognizhi.fyniq.app.
  2. 2Pick the supplier, the bill date and the due date. Fyniq suggests the category and GST code from earlier bills; check them.
  3. 3Attach the bill. Amounts above S$2,000 need an approver before they can be paid.
  4. 4Choose Submit for approval. It appears in the approver's Inbox.
Recommended defaultSelf-approval up to S$2,000 per bill in Founder mode, reviewed weekly by the other founder. Change it in Settings › Approval policies.
Check firstInput tax on overseas digital services follows IRAS's reverse-charge rules. Your accountant confirms the GST code. [verify]
