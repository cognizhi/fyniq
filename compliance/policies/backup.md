# Backup and recovery policy

## Purpose

This policy makes sure we can get our data back after a mistake, a failure or an attack.

## Scope

The books in Fyniq, the documents and evidence attached to them, and any other data the company cannot afford to lose.

## What we do

- We know which data we cannot afford to lose, and where each copy is.
- Backups are kept apart from the systems they protect, so an attack on one does not reach the other.
- We test that a backup can be restored, and that the restored books still balance and their audit trail still
  verifies.
- Records are kept for as long as the law requires.

## What Fyniq enforces

- The database is backed up, with point-in-time recovery, as the platform specifies (CTL-RES-01).
- Restores are drilled, with the ledger and the audit chain verified afterwards (CTL-RES-02).
- Records are kept in retention-locked storage for their retention period (CTL-AUD-03).

## Roles

The Owner makes sure backups run and are tested, and reviews this policy at least once a year.
