Use your own AI plan

Run your own chats with the assistant on an AI plan you already pay for, where the provider allows it. The organisation's connections stay the default.

Your organisation has not turned this on An Owner turns it on in AI settings, with a second Owner, after checking the provider's terms for business data. Ask Mei Lin Tan or Arjun Rao.

How it works

  1. 1An Owner turns it onFor the providers and the kinds of data they choose.
  2. 2You sign in with the providerThe provider asks you to allow Fyniq to use your plan. Fyniq never sees your password, and lists the models your plan allows from the provider.
  3. 3Your chats use your planEach answer shows which model and plan produced it, as every answer does.

Plans you could connect

  • ChatGPT plan Through OpenAI's “Sign in with ChatGPT”, which lets a subscriber use their plan in other apps. It can be connected only if the sign-in lets Fyniq list the plan's models. Owners checking terms
Available when an Owner turns it on.

What your plan would be used for

  • Your own chats with the assistant in Fyniq
  • Never scheduled agents, connected apps or anyone else's chats
  • Only the kinds of data the Owners allow for your plan. Personal data and payroll stay on the organisation's Singapore connection.
  • NRIC and FIN numbers, full bank account numbers and secrets are never sent, whichever plan answers

When your plan runs out

The assistant tells you and asks before it uses the organisation's connection instead. It never switches without asking.

Not supported

Plans whose terms keep them for the provider's own apps, such as Claude Free, Pro and Max, and copying a chat session or browser cookie into Fyniq.
