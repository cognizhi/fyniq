# Ask to hire, get a requisition approved and publish a job post

## What it is

- A requisition asks to hire for a role: the role, the team, new or a replacement, the salary range in Singapore dollars, the start date and whether a pass is expected and, if wished, the hiring manager (a person with a sign-in). The Owner approves it (also when the Owner asked for it); the Recruiter, the HR administrator or the Owner asks for it and writes the job post. The candidate pipeline and offers come later. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. The Recruiter, the HR administrator or the Owner asks for the requisition under Hiring; the Owner approves or rejects it, once.
2. The Recruiter saves a draft post. The wording check lists any age, nationality, sex, marital status, pregnancy, caregiving, race, religion, language, disability or mental-health criterion; it decides nothing.
3. Publishing needs an approved requisition; a flagged criterion stays only with the genuine occupational requirement recorded. A published post never changes.
4. For a role that needs a pass, post the advert on MyCareersFuture yourself and record its day: no offer is made until 14 consecutive days have run, unless an exemption (fewer than 10 employees, an Employment Pass at S$22,500 or more a month, a role of one month or less, a transferee, an S Pass exemption) is recorded with its evidence. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#steps)
5. A change of the occupation, salary, number of vacancies or UEN after the advert needs a new advert: record its day under the renewal form and the 14 days start again; the old advert is kept as evidence. An advert that came down more than 3 months ago cannot be used. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#steps)

## Candidates and interviews

- Once a requisition is approved its card lists candidates on a board: applied, screening, interview, offer, not progressing. Anyone can apply on the careers page; the Recruiter, HR administrator or Owner can add one. Applying is taken as consent to assessing the application; keeping a candidate for future roles needs a separate consent, which can be withdrawn. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#candidates-and-interviews)
- Every move is kept with who made it; not progressing needs a reason; a candidate is hired by the offer, not a move. Notes are the hiring team's and are not released on an access request. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#candidates-and-interviews)
- The interview kit's questions are checked for fair wording like a post, and a flagged question stays only with the genuine requirement recorded. Interviewers read only the candidates assigned to them and score them 1 to 5; they never read the team's notes. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#candidates-and-interviews)
- The HR administrator or the Owner hires a candidate at the offer stage with a start date and the kind of employment: a person record, an onboarding plan from the standard template and a filled requisition result; a pass role waits for the advert days or an exemption from the move to offer onwards; the pass application day is recorded once. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#candidates-and-interviews)
- A candidate not progressing is listed for deletion six months after the decision unless they consented to be kept; a person reviews the report, then the candidate, notes and scores are deleted with a proof; a withdrawn consent restarts the six months. A hired candidate's record (CV, notes, scores, interviewers) is listed six months after the start date the same way; the person record and the hire stay. A waiting report is a warning on Checks, and a failure once its review date has passed. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#candidates-and-interviews)
- To assign an interviewer choose one of the members who hold the Interviewer role under the candidate and select Assign. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#candidates-and-interviews)

## Interviewing, the line manager and a candidate's own data

- An interviewer opens My interviews: only the candidates assigned to them, with the role, the stage and the kit's criteria; they read the CV and save a score from 1 to 5 with what it rests on. A score is kept as written; the later score for a criterion counts. They never see the team's notes, another interviewer's scores or any other candidate. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#interviewing-the-candidates-assigned-to-you)
- A line manager sees, under Roles I am hiring for, the requisitions that name them as hiring manager, with where each stands, its post's dates and how many candidates are at each stage: no salary and no candidate's name. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#for-the-line-manager)
- A candidate may ask to see their data. The Recruiter, the HR administrator or the Owner makes a link under the candidate (1 to 30 days, 14 by default), shown once, and sends it themselves. With it and no sign-in the candidate sees what they gave, the role, where they stand and when their data is due for deletion, never the team's notes or scores, and may withdraw the consent to be kept for future roles. A link can be revoked at once; a lost link needs a new one. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#letting-a-candidate-see-their-own-data)

## The careers page

- The Recruiter, the HR administrator or the Owner chooses an address, the company's name and some words and switches the page on; anyone then reads /careers/<address> with no sign-in: each published post's title, team, text and day, never a salary or a person. No other company can use the address. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#the-careers-page)

## When it looks wrong

- A post refused for wording needs rewording or a recorded requirement; a word the check flags may be fine, and a person judges it. "Moved on" means the requisition was decided or the post published already. Only the Recruiter, the HR officer, the HR administrator and the Owner see Hiring; a line manager sees their own roles and an interviewer their candidates. A candidate's link that says it is not valid has expired or been revoked: make a new one. No interviewer to choose means nobody holds the Interviewer role yet. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/hiring.md#common-problems)
