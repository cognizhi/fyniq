# Give the privacy notices and record consent

## What it is

Under the PDPA the company tells people what it does with their personal data. **Privacy notices and consent** (from
**Compliance**, Expert mode) holds two notices, and the consent for any purpose that needs it:
- the **members' notice**, for the people who use Fyniq: what the company keeps about them, why, who sees it, how long,
  and what they can ask;
- the **contacts' notice**, for the people the company deals with at its customers and suppliers.

The Owner, the Auditor and the Compliance officer see the page. The Owner and the Compliance officer publish the
notices.

## Publish a notice

Fyniq starts each notice as a draft written from the PDPC's guides. Clauses marked [counsel to confirm] bear on the law:
have counsel confirm them before you publish.

1. Select **Draft** beside a notice.
2. Adopt the draft as it is, or edit the text and select **Save the draft**.
3. Select **Publish version**.

A published version never changes: to change a notice, save a new draft and publish it.

Once a version is published:
- every member is asked to read and accept the members' notice, on **Home** and in the **Inbox**, under **Acknowledge
  policy**; a member who has just joined finds it there first;
- every email Fyniq sends to a customer or supplier ends with the first paragraph of the contacts' notice and the Data
  Protection Officer's contact. Contacts do not acknowledge their notice.

## Consent

Fyniq uses members' and contacts' data for the work, the business relationship and the records the law requires, which
need no consent [counsel to confirm]. No purpose is defined at first.

If the company uses personal data for something more, such as a newsletter, the Compliance officer adds a purpose under
**Purposes and consent**: what it is, what it means for the person, whose data it uses, and whether it needs consent. A
purpose never changes once added.

For a purpose that needs consent, select **Record** to record that a person gave it, or withdrew it, and how. A consent
is given only when none stands, and withdrawn only when one does. Withdrawal stops further use. Every consent stays in
the list, with its withdrawal, and in the audit trail.
