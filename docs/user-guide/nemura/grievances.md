# Raise a concern, and handle one

## What it is

Anyone who works here can raise a concern in confidence about something at work that is not right. It is read only by one HR administrator or the Owner, who looks into it and writes down what they found. The people it is about can never read it, whatever role they hold, and nobody else can either: not another HR administrator, not the Owner if they are not the one handling it, not the assistant. The Workplace Fairness Act, which asks employers for a grievance process, has not started yet; this channel is ready before it does.

## Raise a concern

1. Open **Raise a concern**.
2. Write what happened, when and who was there, in 20 to 4000 characters.
3. If it is about particular people, choose them (up to five). Leave it empty when it is about how things are done.
4. Select **Send in confidence**.

You are told who is looking into it. If everyone who could is the person who raised it or is involved, it waits, and you will see that it has been taken once someone who is not involved takes it. You cannot be the person it is about, and you cannot handle your own.

## Read the outcome

Under **Your concerns** you see where each one is: waiting, being looked into, or decided. When it is decided you read the written outcome and what the inquiry concluded: substantiated, partly substantiated, not substantiated or no finding. You do not see the inquiry's notes.

## Handle a grievance

Only an HR administrator or the Owner handles one, and only one they are not involved in.

1. Open **Grievances**. **Yours** lists what is assigned to you; **Waiting for someone who is not involved** lists those nobody could handle at the start, which you may take if you are neither the one who raised it nor involved.
2. Open one to read it, who raised it, who it is about and the inquiry so far. Reading it is recorded.
3. Add notes, interviews and findings with **Add**. Entries are never changed or removed.
4. When you have decided, write the outcome and choose what the inquiry concludes, then select **Write the outcome**. It is written once; the person who raised it can then read it.
5. If you cannot handle it, select **Pass it on** and say why. It goes to the next eligible HR administrator or the Owner who is not the one who raised it and not involved; the reason goes in the inquiry. You no longer read it.

## Common problems

- **It says there is no such grievance.** You may not read it: you did not raise it, you do not handle it, or you are involved in it. Nobody is told which.
- **Nobody else can handle it.** Every other HR administrator and the Owner raised it or are involved; it stays with you.
- **The outcome is already written.** An outcome is written once, and the record is closed after it.
