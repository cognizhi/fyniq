# Claim GST back on a bad debt

When an invoice that bore GST is written off as a bad debt, the GST can be claimed back from IRAS in the GST return. **Sales › Bad debts** lists each such write-off with the GST in the part written off, the first day a claim may be made and the last, and where it stands.

(src: docs/user-guide/bad-debts.md#what-it-is)

## The dates

- A claim may be made once twelve months have passed from the date of supply, or from the write-off when the debtor became insolvent sooner.
- It must be made within five years from the date of supply (seven for a supply before 1 January 2007), the last day being the day before the anniversary; the accountant confirms this.
- Not yet, Claim now and Out of time say where each debt stands; the dates come from the statutory pack.

(src: docs/user-guide/bad-debts.md#the-dates)

## Claiming the relief

On a row that says Claim now, Claim relief opens a form: the day you claim (within the dates above, and not after today), the last day of the GST return period the claim goes in, and a note about the steps taken to recover the debt or the debtor's insolvency. The conditions that are not dates are your declaration. The tax agent, the accountant and the finance controller can claim.

The claim posts at once: it takes the invoice's GST in the part written off out of the GST control account and puts it against the account the debt was written off to, in Singapore dollars. Each write-off is claimed once, and an invoice that a credit note credits cannot be claimed here. A wrong claim is voided (reversed) and made again; a write-off with a claim cannot be reversed until its claim is voided. A debt repaid after the claim and an insolvent debtor's date are not built yet.

(src: docs/user-guide/bad-debts.md#claiming-the-relief)
