5 · Revenue

Saved 10:41 · Grace Ho

The company earns revenue from software subscriptions and from data projects. Subscriptions are billed monthly or yearly in advance and recognised evenly over the subscription period; amounts billed for later months are deferred.

Fixed-fee projects are recognised when each milestone is accepted by the customer. Time-and-materials projects are recognised as the hours are worked, at the agreed rates.
