# A person's record and job history in Nemura

## What it is

- A record holds name, contact, start and any leaving date, the job history and, for the people team, the legal classification.
- A job change ends the old record the day the new one starts, so who held which job under whom is known for any day. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/person.md#what-it-is)

## Who may read it

- The people team, the person themselves, or their manager for a report. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/person.md#before-you-start)

## What it shows

1. **Contact**, then **Job history**: from, to, job title, department, employment type and worksite.
2. The people team also sees **Legal classification**: contract of service or for service, Employment Act cover, Part IV class, residency and date of birth. A contractor, a contract for service, is not covered by the Employment Act.

3. The HR officer, the HR administrator, the Payroll administrator and the Owner also see **Key employment terms**, which the Employment Act asks the employer to give in writing. Only the Payroll administrator and the Owner see and write the pay. See [[nemura.letters.kets]].
4. A person's pay record, the year to date and one-off items are kept under **Payroll** by the Payroll administrator and the Owner alone; see [[nemura.payroll-records]].

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/person.md#steps)

## When it looks wrong

- "No such person, or you may not see their record" does not say which; ask the people team. See [[nemura.people]].
- No classification: it is not set, or the reader is not in the people team. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/person.md#common-problems)
