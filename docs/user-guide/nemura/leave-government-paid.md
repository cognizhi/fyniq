# Record government-paid leave and track the employer's claim

## What it is

**Government-paid leave** lists each block of maternity, paternity or shared parental leave an employee takes for a child, with
the day the employer's claim must be filed by and the claims filed for it. The Government reimburses part of the pay for these
schemes. A person files the employer's claim on the **Government-Paid Leave portal**; Nemura keeps the record and moves no
money, and the salary owed never depends on the reimbursement.

## Before you start

- You need to be the HR officer or the HR administrator to record leave and claims. The people team, the Owner and the Payroll
  administrator can read the page.
- The child must be declared among the employee's dependants, with a date of birth: see [A person's record](person.md).
- The rules come from the country pack for the child's birth date. For a child born in 2026: maternity leave is 16 weeks
  (the employer pays the first 8 weeks for a first or second child, and the Government reimburses the last 8 weeks, capped at
  S$10,000 for each 4 weeks and S$20,000 in all); paternity leave is 4 weeks, reimbursed up to S$2,500 a week and S$10,000
  in all; shared parental leave is 10 weeks for children born from 1 April 2026 and 6 weeks for children born from 1 April 2025
  to 31 March 2026, reimbursed up to S$2,500 a week. The child must be a Singapore Citizen, and the employee must have served
  3 months before the birth. The values were read from MOM's and MSF's pages and have not yet been checked by a second person.

## Steps

1. Open **Government-paid leave** and, under **Record a block of leave**, choose the person, the child, the scheme, the child's
   order and the first and last day. Maternity leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the birth; paternity and shared parental
   leave begin on or after the birth and end within 12 months of it. A continuous block counts every calendar day.
2. Nemura refuses a block that is more than the scheme's weeks, falls outside its window, or overlaps annual leave, sick leave or
   other government-paid leave of the person. Each block shows its **claim deadline**: 3 months after the last day.
3. After a person files the claim on the portal, select **Record claim** on the block: the amount, the day and the portal's
   reference. An amount above what the scheme's cap suggests is recorded and flagged, never refused.
4. When the portal answers, record it under the claim: a **Received** amount (partial receipts are fine) or a **Rejected**
   answer. The claim shows paid in full, part paid, rejected or filed, waiting.

## Common problems

- **The child is not a Singapore Citizen.** Government-paid leave is for a citizen child; the employer pays any leave the
  company gives.
- **The person served fewer than 3 months before the birth.** The scheme asks for 3 months.
- **The deadline is near.** What needs a look lists a block with no claim filed within 30 days of its deadline.
- **The child has no date of birth yet.** Record it under the person's dependants first.
