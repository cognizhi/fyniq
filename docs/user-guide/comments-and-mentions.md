# Comment on a record and mention a colleague

## What it is

A comment is a note left on a record: an invoice, a credit note, a bill, a vendor credit, a journal, an expense claim, a quote, a sales
order, a purchase order or a contact. Colleagues who may see the record read it there. Type **@** and the start of a name to mention a colleague, and
Fyniq tells them.

## Who sees a comment, and who you can mention

You read and write comments on a record only if you may see the record. A record you may not see is not found, as if it did not exist. The names
Fyniq offers when you type **@** are the members who may see this record, and only them: a colleague who may not open it is never offered, and cannot
be mentioned. An Approver who acts for some departments sees the records of those departments, so they are offered, and told, for those only.

## Steps

1. Open the record and find **Comments**: on a contact it is the **Activity** tab; on a bill, an invoice or a journal, select **Show comments**.
2. Write in **Add a comment**, up to 2,000 characters.
3. To mention someone, type **@** and the start of a name, choose them with the arrow keys and **Enter** (or select the name), and go on writing.
4. Select **Add comment**. The comment is kept on the record, and each person you mentioned is told in their notifications. You are not told of your own.

## Good to know

- A comment is never changed or removed, so a mistake is answered with another comment. The record's history keeps who wrote what and when.
- Only the people you mention are told, and only if they may see the record.
- A mention that cannot be made, such as someone who may not see the record, is refused with the same message as a name that is no member's.
- Mentions are notifications of the type **Someone mentioned you in a comment**, in the daily digest by email unless you choose otherwise under **Notifications**.
