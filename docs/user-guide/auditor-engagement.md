# Work with the auditor's engagement

## Find it

In Expert mode, open **Reports** and choose **Auditor engagement**. An Owner opens an engagement for the auditor, the auditor asks and samples, and an Owner, Controller or Accountant answers.

## Open an engagement

An Owner opens an engagement for a member who holds the **Auditor** role: a name, the firm, the period under audit, and the dates between which the auditor may add requests and samples, up to 366 days. It stays **pending** until a second Owner confirms it. In Founder mode the same Owner may confirm it. An Owner can close it earlier; a closed engagement is never reopened, so open a new one to go on.

## Requests

While the engagement is active and between its dates, the auditor **asks** for an item and names the Owner, Controller or Accountant who answers it. That person, or an Owner, answers with a note and with evidence files already added to the books. The auditor reads the answer and **accepts and closes** the request. Nobody else writes to the engagement.

## Samples

The auditor **draws a sample** of posted ledger lines: a period inside the audit period, an account if they choose, the smallest line and how many lines, up to 200. The lines are picked by a seed that is kept with the sample, so the same seed over the same ledger draws the same lines and nothing is chosen by hand. Each line shows where it posted from, which opens read-only. A sample is kept as it was drawn and never changes.

## What the auditor cannot do

The auditor's one right to write is on these requests and samples. Everything else they open is read-only, and payroll personal data stays hidden unless it is granted. Every request, answer and sample is in the audit trail.
