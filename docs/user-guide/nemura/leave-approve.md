# Approve or refuse leave

## What it is

**Team leave** lists the leave requests waiting for your decision and the ones decided lately. A line manager sees and
decides on their reports' requests. The HR officer, the HR administrator and the Owner see and decide on anyone's. The
Payroll administrator can read them. Nobody decides their own request: another approver does.

## Before you start

- You need to be a line manager, the HR officer, the HR administrator or the Owner.
- Your reports are the people whose manager you are on the reporting line.

## Steps

1. Open **Team leave**.
2. Under **Waiting for a decision**, read the person, the dates, the days and the note.
3. Select **Approve**. To refuse, write the reason in the box beside the buttons first, then select **Refuse**. A refusal
   without a reason is not accepted: the person is told why.
4. The decision is audited, the person is told, and a refusal gives the reserved days back to their balance.

## Common problems

- **A request is not in the list.** You see only the requests of the people who report to you, unless you are in the
  people team.
- **It says the request is approved already.** A decision is made once. To change it, the person cancels the request, or
  the people team corrects it.
- **You cannot decide your own request.** Ask another approver.
