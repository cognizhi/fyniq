# Read Nemura's country pack and set the leave policy against it

## What it is

- The **Country pack** page lists the statutory facts for Singapore, each with the official page, the day it was read and what it said.
- A **researched** value was read from the official page but not checked by a second person; the **Leave policy** sits on top of the statute. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/packs.md#what-it-is)

## Who and what first

- The HR administrator or the Owner accepts a version, records a decision or sets the policy; the HR officer, the Payroll administrator and the Administrator read.
- Accepting a version verifies nothing; the publisher does. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/packs.md#before-you-start)

## Steps

1. Open **Country pack** and read the sources.
2. Give the company's reason for each researched value and select **Record decision**; a calculation that needs one has no result until then.
3. Open **Leave policy**, enter the start day and the entitlements, then select **Set policy**. See [[nemura.people]] for the people record.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/packs.md#steps)

## When it is refused

- A policy that gives less than the statute is refused, naming each rule; one that gives more is accepted with a note.
- **What needs a look** lists a pass about to expire, next year's holidays missing from 1 December, and researched values in use with no decision.
- A worksite outside Singapore is refused. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/packs.md#common-problems)
