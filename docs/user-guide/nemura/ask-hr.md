# Ask the people team a question

## What it is

**Ask a question** sends a written question to the people team about leave, your record or anything else about your work. Someone other than you answers it, within
3 working days, and the answer shows under **My requests**.

## Before you start

- Sign in first: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).
- Your login must be linked to your person record.

## Steps

1. Open **Ask a question**.
2. Write your question, up to 1,000 characters, and select **Send the question**. Nemura shows the day it is due.
3. Follow it under **Where my requests are** in [My requests](requests.md). When it is answered, its step is **Ready** and the reply is there.

## What happens next

- A question is answered once. If it needs more, ask a new question.
- You can **Withdraw** a question that nobody has answered.

## Common problems

- **The question is refused.** It is empty or longer than 1,000 characters: shorten it.
- **For a letter, a change of details or flexible work,** use their own pages: see [Ask for a letter](letters-request.md) and [Change your own details](my-details.md).
