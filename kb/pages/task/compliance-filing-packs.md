# Work out a financial year's tax for the tax agent

**Compliance › Year-end tax** works out a financial year's corporate income tax from the books: the profit before tax and the revenue come from the ledger, the adjustments from you with their reasons, and the exemption, the rate and the rebate from the statutory packs. It files nothing and keeps nothing.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Enter the day the financial year ends, choose the partial tax exemption or the start-up tax exemption (a qualifying company's first three Years of Assessment), and tick the cash grant box only if the company met its local employee condition.
2. Select **Add an adjustment** for each amount to add back or take off, with the reason, then **Work it out**.
3. Read the form the revenue calls for: Form C-S (Lite) up to S$200,000 of revenue, Form C-S up to S$5 million, otherwise Form C; a company within the Lite limit may file Form C-S too. A Year of Assessment with no announced rebate has none.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#work-out-a-year)

## Statutory updates

A newer version of an activated pack is listed with the rows it would add, remove or change; accepting it is done in Settings › Statutory packs, by two people, and re-posts nothing.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#statutory-updates)
