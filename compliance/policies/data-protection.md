# Data protection policy

## Purpose

This policy says how the company handles personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act. It follows the PDPC's
guide to a data protection management programme: govern it, set policies, run the processes, and review them.

## Scope

All personal data the company collects, uses, discloses or keeps: about its people, its customers' and suppliers'
contacts, and anyone else.

## What we do

- **Accountability.** [counsel to confirm] The company names a Data Protection Officer, the Compliance officer in
  Fyniq, whose business contact information is available to the public.
- **Purpose and consent.** [counsel to confirm] We collect personal data for purposes we have told people about and
  that a reasonable person would consider appropriate, with their consent unless the law allows otherwise.
- **NRIC numbers.** [counsel to confirm] We collect NRIC numbers, or copies of NRICs, only where the law requires it or
  where it is necessary to verify someone's identity to a high degree of accuracy. We never use an NRIC number as a
  password or to sign anyone in.
- **Protection.** We protect personal data with reasonable security arrangements, and limit who can see it.
- **Retention.** [counsel to confirm] We keep personal data only as long as its purpose or the law needs it, then
  delete or anonymise it.
- **Access and correction.** [counsel to confirm] We answer requests to see or correct personal data as the Act
  requires.
- **Transfers.** [counsel to confirm] Personal data leaves Singapore only with protection comparable to the Act's, and
  each transfer is recorded.
- **Impact assessments.** We assess the data protection impact of a new way of handling personal data before we start.
- **Breaches.** We follow the incident response policy.

## What Fyniq enforces

- Sensitive fields are encrypted and masked (CTL-DP-02), and never written to logs or AI traces (CTL-DP-03).
- No sign-in, step-up or recovery uses an NRIC number, and no file password derives from one (CTL-DP-04).
- The data inventory, retention schedule and impact assessments are kept current (CTL-DP-05).
- Data stays in Singapore unless a transfer is documented (CTL-DP-07).

## Roles

The Data Protection Officer owns this policy, reviews it at least once a year, and is the contact for anyone with a
question about their personal data.
