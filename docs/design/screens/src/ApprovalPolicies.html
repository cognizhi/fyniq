The first rule that matches a bill decides its steps.

WhenAmount ≤ S$2,000.00

2 · S$2,000.01 to S$10,000.00
WhenAmount > S$2,000.00Amount ≤ S$10,000.00
Then
  1. 1Role: Approver, 1 of 2Never the person who prepared the bill
3 · Above S$10,000.00 or a new vendorChanged in v8
WhenAmount > S$10,000.00orVendor first billed in the last 90 days
Then
  1. 1Role: Approver, 1 of 2Never the person who prepared the bill
  2. 2Named person: Grace HoNew in v8Reviews before payment; any one reviewer is enough

Conditions can use amount, entity, department, account, vendor and risk tier. Steps name a role or a person, with a quorum.

Fixed for every policy. A bill the assistant prepared goes through the same steps, and the person who asked it counts as the preparer. The AI proposal policy always keeps a human approval step; it cannot be removed or set to approve by itself.
