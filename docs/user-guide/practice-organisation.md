# Try a task in a practice organisation

## What it is

A practice organisation is a separate organisation, labelled **Practice**, where you can try a task without touching your real books. It starts with empty
books and nothing copied from your company. Only an Owner of an organisation opens one, and only one at a time.

## Open one

1. Open **Settings → Organisation** and find **Practice organisation**.
2. Select **Open a practice organisation**, and confirm it's you if Fyniq asks.
3. The practice organisation appears under **Your organisations**. Select **Switch** beside it.

Every page in it shows **Practice organisation** at the top. Nothing there is real. To go back, select **Back to** your company in that banner, or **Switch** beside your
company under **Settings → Organisation**.

## Good to know

- You are the Owner of your practice organisation, and nobody else is a member. It does not see your company's data, and your company does not see its.
- You hold one practice organisation at a time: **Open a practice organisation** is off while you hold one. You cannot open one from a practice organisation.
- Opening one is recorded in the audit trail of both organisations.
- A practice organisation is not deleted by itself, and Fyniq has no way yet to delete an organisation: until one is built, it stays, and you cannot open another while you hold it.
