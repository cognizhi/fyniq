# Use your account after you leave

## What it is

When your last day has passed, the same sign-in opens **My documents**: a narrow account that keeps your own documents and letters, and a way to ask the people team
for a copy of one you lost. It holds no role, whatever you held before, and nothing else in Nemura is open to you. It ends 12 months after your last day, which is the
period the company keeps a former employee's records.

## Before you start

- Sign in the way you did before: with your company email through your provider, or with your passkey. See [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).

## Steps

1. Sign in. **My documents** opens and says when your account ends.
2. Open a document under **Documents on my file**, or a letter under **My letters**. Select **Make a link that works for seven days** to share one letter.
3. To ask for a copy of a document you lost, write which one under **Ask for a copy of a lost document** and select **Send the question**.

## What happens next

- The people team answers within 3 working days. For a letter or your key employment terms they ask for it again as a new copy, and it shows under **My letters** once it is signed.
- When your account ends, you can no longer sign in. The records follow the retention rules.

## Common problems

- **Nemura says that is not part of your account now.** After your last day only your documents, letters and a question to the people team are open.
- **My last day is wrong.** Ask the people team to correct the date; the account follows it.
- **I cannot sign in.** The 12 months after your last day have passed. Ask the company for a copy of what you need.
