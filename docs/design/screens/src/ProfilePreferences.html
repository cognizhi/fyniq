Experience

Mode Basic shows the essentials; Expert adds accounts, codes and grid tools. Switching keeps your page and drafts, and never changes what you can do. Owners start in Basic.
Ledger wording
Plain view says money in, money out and category; Accountant view says debit, credit and account. Both show the same figures. Basic mode always uses Plain view. This applies when you switch to Expert.
Theme Your organisation's default is System. Your choice wins on your account.
Density Compact fits more rows on screen: 32 px rows instead of 40 px.
Reduce motion Nothing fades or slides for you: pages, panels and lists appear in their final state at once.
Organisation motion is On. If an Administrator turns it off, nothing moves for anyone and this switch has no effect. Your device's reduced-motion setting always wins.
