# Acceptable use policy

## Purpose

This policy says how people may use the company's devices, accounts and data, so that everyday work does not put them
at risk.

## Scope

Everyone who uses a company device or account, or a personal device for company work.

## What we expect

- Use company accounts for company work, and keep your sign-in and second factor to yourself.
- Keep phones and laptops used for work locked, updated and encrypted; install apps only from the official stores.
- Do not copy company or personal data to personal accounts, chat apps or unapproved cloud services.
- Do not put personal data, NRIC numbers, bank details or secrets into AI tools, emails or chat unless the work needs
  it and the tool is approved for it.
- Report a lost device, a suspicious message or a mistake at once: reporting early is never held against you.

## What Fyniq enforces

- Every member signs in with a second factor, and Fyniq checks each night that every one has one (CTL-IAM-01).
- Logs and traces never keep NRIC numbers, bank numbers or secrets (CTL-DP-03).

## Roles

Every member acknowledges this policy when it is published and when they join. The Owner reviews it at least once a
year.
