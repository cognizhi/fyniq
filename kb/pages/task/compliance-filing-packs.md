# Work out a financial year's tax for the tax agent

**Compliance › Year-end tax** works out a financial year's corporate income tax from the books: the profit before tax and the revenue come from the ledger, the adjustments from you with their reasons, and the exemption, the rate and the rebate from the statutory packs. It files nothing; the adjustments can be kept with their evidence as a paper.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Enter the day the financial year ends, choose the partial tax exemption or the start-up tax exemption (a qualifying company's first three Years of Assessment), and tick the cash grant box only if the company met its local employee condition.
2. Select **Add an adjustment** for each amount to add back or take off, with the reason, then **Work it out**.
3. Read the form the revenue calls for: Form C-S (Lite) up to S$200,000 of revenue, Form C-S up to S$5 million, otherwise Form C; a company within the Lite limit may file Form C-S too. A Year of Assessment with no announced rebate has none.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#work-out-a-year)

## Keep and record the paper

Keep as a draft paper saves the year, the exemption and the adjustments with their reasons under Kept papers. Attach what supports them to the draft (the invoices of the non-deductible expenses, the capital allowance schedule, the tax agent's note), then Record the paper: a paper with adjustments needs at least one file, the figures it worked out then are kept with it, and it changes no more. The latest recorded paper of a year is the one in force; keeping a new paper supersedes the earlier. Recording posts and files nothing.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#keep-and-record-the-paper)

## Statutory updates

A newer version of an activated pack is listed with the rows it would add, remove or change; accepting it is done in Settings › Statutory packs, by two people, and re-posts nothing.

(src: docs/user-guide/filing-packs.md#statutory-updates)
