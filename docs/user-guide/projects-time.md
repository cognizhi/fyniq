# Log your time and approve your team's

## What it is

**My time** (and **Projects, Timesheet**) is where you log the time you spend on projects, a week at a time, and where a
project's manager approves it. Time is whole minutes, shown as hours and minutes. Time posts nothing to the books: salaries are
already in the ledger through payroll, and time is how Fyniq shows what a project's staff cost.

You log time only on a project you are on, on one of its tasks in use. Time on an internal project is never billable. Only you see
and change your own time.

## Log your week

1. Open **My time**. Choose **Add a row**: a project, then a task.
2. Type the time in each day: 2:15 or 2.25 both mean 2 hours 15 minutes. Add a note for the row if you like.
3. Select **Save the week**. You can save a week as often as you like while it is a draft.

Working days up to today with no time entered show a warning under **Days with no time**, less public holidays.

## Use the timer

Choose a project and a task, then **Start the timer**. Select **Stop and add the time** when you finish: its time becomes a draft entry
on the day it started, to the nearest minute, at least one. One timer runs at a time. A timer that ran more than a day is refused: drop
it and enter the time by hand.

## Send the week for approval

Select **Send the week for approval**. Each project's part of the week goes to that project's managers. Submitted time is locked. A
manager approves it, which locks it for good, or returns it with a comment: then the entries are yours to change again, and you
send the week again.

## Approve the team's time

**Projects, Timesheet** lists the weeks waiting for you under **Time to approve**. Open one to see its entries, then **Approve the
week** or **Return with a comment** saying what to fix.

You can approve a week when you hold the **Approver** role and are a manager of its project, or when you are the **Controller**, who
approves any project's time. Naming a person a manager gives them no right of its own. **Nobody approves their own time**: another
manager of the project, or the Controller, does.

## Correct approved time

Approved time never changes. Under **Correct approved time**, select **Correct it** on an entry: Fyniq adds a reversing entry with the
opposite time. Enter the right time as a new entry and send the week again; the manager approves both.

## Days with no time

A working day up to today with no time shows as missing, less the public holidays of Singapore's statutory calendar. Enter the time, or
ignore a day you did not work.

**On Friday afternoon** (4 pm, Singapore time) Fyniq sends each person on an active project a notice naming the weekdays of the week with no time
entered, public holidays excluded, once for the week. It is yours alone: nobody sees whose time is missing through it. You can turn this type of notice
off in your notification settings, as any other. Nothing is sent while Projects and time are switched off.
