# See your leave balance and how it was worked out

## What it is

**My leave** shows your leave for a year: what you are **entitled** to, what you have **taken**, what is **booked** for
leave not yet taken, what **expired** and what is **left**. Under the table, **How it was worked out** lists each step of
the calculation and where the days come from: the country pack and your company's policy. The leave year is the
calendar year. Annual leave is counted in your working days, set by your **working pattern**.

## Before you start

- Sign in first: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).
- Leave must be switched on for your organisation. If it is off, the page says so; the Owner or the HR administrator
  switches it on under **Features**.

## Steps

1. Select **My leave**. The page opens on this year.
2. Read the table, then **How it was worked out**. The statute's annual leave days are pro-rated by your completed
   months of service in the year, then rounded to a whole day: under one half down, a half or more up.
3. Select **Previous year** or **Next year** to see another year. Under the table, **The month at a glance** shows you and your team
   for a month: a letter marks away, leave, a request waiting, sick leave or a public holiday. Where you may not see the type
   of someone's leave it says **Away**.
4. Unused annual leave is carried into the next year and can be taken until the end of the twelfth month after the year
   ends; days you take in the new year use the carried days first, and what is not used then expires.

## For the people team

- Open a person's record and select **Leave and working pattern** to see their leave. The HR officer and the HR
  administrator can then select **Work out annual leave** to record the year's entitlement (run again, it writes only
  the difference, as a correction), **Carry unused days forward**, and **Set pattern** to start a new working pattern
  on a day. A working pattern is history: setting a new one ends the old one the day before.
- Maternity, paternity and shared parental leave are recorded separately, with the employer's reimbursement claim: see
  [Record government-paid leave](leave-government-paid.md).
- Nothing is edited or deleted. Every grant, correction, carry-forward and expiry is a new entry in the person's leave
  ledger, and each is audited.

## Common problems

- **No leave has been granted for this year yet.** The people team has not worked out the year's annual leave.
- **The person has no legal classification.** Set it on their record first; the statute that applies depends on it.
- **No country pack covers the year.** Choose a year from 2026 on.
- **You started less than three months ago.** Annual leave starts when you have completed three months of service, and
  the months worked before then count.
