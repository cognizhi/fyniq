# Director accounts

## What it is

Accounting › **Director accounts** shows what the company owes each director and shareholder,
and what they owe it. The amounts sit on two accounts:

- **2500 Loan from director / shareholder:** money a founder lent the company, and company costs
  they paid themselves and kept as a loan;
- **2120 Deferred remuneration payable:** pay and directors' fees owed but not yet paid.

Every amount on these accounts names the person it belongs to and where it came from: lent, paid
personally, pay deferred, director's fee, paid out to them, or other. That is how each person's
balance adds up to the accounts, and how the accountant tells a loan from unpaid pay, which are
taxed differently.

A person whose amounts add up to a debit owes the company. The card says **Account in debit**,
and what that means depends on the company's exempt-private-company status (below).

## Mark the founders' contacts

Each director or shareholder is a contact. In **Contacts**, open the contact and tick **A
director**, **A shareholder** or both. A contact can also be a customer or a vendor. Once an
amount on 2500 or 2120 names a contact, the contact stays a director or shareholder, so its
history always names a person.

## Link each director to the member who signs in as them

On their card, select **Link member** and choose the member. The link asks for approval: another
person who holds the approval right on director accounts approves it, never the member being
linked. Once linked, the member sees their own account in **Money I put in**, and never approves
their own settlement. A member signs in as one director or shareholder at most. To unlink them,
choose **Nobody**.

## Record the exempt private company status

An exempt private company has no corporate shareholder and at most 20 members. The top of the page
says the status in force. To record it:

1. Select **Record exempt status**, then the day it applies from and whether the company is
   exempt.
2. Attach the ACRA business profile that shows it.
3. Select **Record the status**.

An Owner or the Controller records it. A recorded status never changes: when it changes, for
example when a corporate investor joins, record a new one from that day.

When a director's account is in debit:

- **Exempt:** a loan to a director is allowed, and it is disclosed in the financial statements and
  to investors.
- **Not exempt, or no status recorded:** section 162 of the Companies Act bans loans to directors.
  Advances for company expenses are allowed when approved in general meeting, or repaid within 6
  months after the next AGM. Repay it, or ask your accountant.

## Record a pay choice

Each card shows the person's **pay choice** in force this month: no salary, salary paid, salary
deferred, director's fee, or shares instead of pay. With none recorded, it is **No salary**.

1. Select **Change pay choice**, then the month it applies from and the choice.
2. Attach the signed decision: the board minute or directors' resolution.
3. Select **Record the pay choice**.

An Owner or the Controller records it. A recorded choice never changes: to change it, record a new
one from the month it changes, and the new one applies from then. Salary deferred still means CPF
is due at the end of each month and the salary is taxed, though it is not paid, so ask your
accountant first. Payroll applies the choice once it is built.

## Record a director's fee

A fee the shareholders voted to a director is recorded on their card:

1. Select **Fees**, then enter the day of the general meeting that voted it, the fee and the
   period it is for.
2. Attach the shareholders' resolution or the meeting's minutes.
3. Select **Ask for approval**.

An Owner or the Controller records it. Another person who holds the approval right approves it,
never the director it is paid to. On approval it posts to 6050 Directors' fees on the day it was
voted, and the director's account on 2120 shows it as owed to them until it is paid. A posted fee
never changes: correct it with a journal to the director's account.

## Settle a balance

What the company owes a director or shareholder is settled from their card:

1. Select **Settle**, then choose how: **Repay in cash**, **Keep as a loan**, **Convert to
   shares** or **Waive**. Converting to a SAFE or note comes later.
2. Choose the account it is owed on and the amount, at most what the company owes them, then
   the details: a loan's interest and repayment day, or the account a waiver credits, the
   capacity they waive it in and why. Select **Save the draft**.
3. Check what it posts, then attach the agreement: the loan note or board minute, or the deed of
   waiver.
4. Select **Ask for approval**.

An Owner or the Controller settles a balance. Another person who holds the approval right approves
it, never the person settled. If every other person who could approve also benefits from a
settlement of their own, the accountant reviews it as well. A loan kept posts nothing. A
repayment is paid by the next payment batch into the person's approved bank account. A waiver
posts on the day it is approved.

## Convert a balance to shares

Converting is approved first and becomes effective once ACRA registers the shares:

1. In **Settle**, choose **Convert to shares**, the class, the price per share and its basis,
   and the capacity they convert in. Fyniq rounds down to whole shares, and what is left stays
   owed to them.
2. Attach all four documents, each as its type: the shareholders' approval, the board
   resolution, the conversion agreement and their balance confirmation. Then select **Ask for
   approval**. Approving it posts nothing.
3. Once ACRA's register of members shows the allotment, enter ACRA's lodgement reference, the
   resolutions and the day it was registered, and select **Record as registered**. The
   conversion posts on that day, never earlier, and the shares join the register of share
   issues.

The shareholders' approval lapses on the date by which the next AGM must be held. After that
day, a conversion not yet registered needs a new approval. A day in a closed period is refused:
reopen the period rather than choose a later date. The accountant chooses the measurement
basis. The default is the carrying amount; the shares' fair value, with the difference in profit
or loss, needs their note.

## Name the person on a journal or opening balance

On a journal line or an opening balance on 2500 or 2120, choose **Whose account** and **Where it
came from**. A balance owed to two founders is two lines, one each. A journal that leaves the person
out does not post, and neither does one that names a person on any other account.

When you categorise a bank line to 2500, choose the founder as the line's contact: money in is
money they lent, and money out is money paid out to them.

## Year-end confirmations

After each financial year ends, every director and shareholder confirms their balances at the
year's end, in **Money I put in**, with their passkey. Each card says:

- **Confirmed:** the balances at the year's end were confirmed and are still what the books show.
- **Changed since confirmed:** an entry dated in that year was posted after the confirmation; the
  person confirms again.
- **Not confirmed:** nobody has confirmed yet. A director with no member linked cannot confirm:
  link the member first.

The close of a financial year's last month warns of every director whose balances are not
confirmed.

## Read a statement

Select **Statement** on a person's card and choose the dates. For each account it shows the
opening balance, the amounts by where they came from, and the closing balance, with what the
company owes the person shown positive. **Every movement** lists each line, with its document.

## Send it for a balance confirmation

Select **PDF for confirmation** to download the statement as a PDF to send to the person, or
**CSV** for its movements. Each download is recorded in the audit trail.

## Common problems

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| Account 2500 is a director's account, so the line names whose it is | Choose the director or shareholder the amount belongs to, and where it came from |
| The person is not a director or shareholder of this organisation | Mark the contact as a director or shareholder, or choose another |
| Account is not a director's account, so the line names no person | Leave the person out, or post to the director's account |
| Amounts on 2500 and 2120 name no director or shareholder | Reverse those journals and post them again with the person each amount belongs to |
| You are the member being linked, so another person approves it | Ask another Owner or the Controller to approve the link |
| The pay choice has no signed decision attached | Attach the board minute or resolution, then record it |
| The pay choice is recorded, so it changes no more | Record a new choice from the month it changes |
| The fee has no shareholders' resolution attached | Attach the resolution or the minutes, then ask for approval |
| The status has no evidence attached | Attach the ACRA business profile, then record it |
| The status is recorded, so it changes no more | Record a new status from the day it changes |
| The fee is paid to you, so another person approves it | Ask another Owner or the Controller to approve it |
