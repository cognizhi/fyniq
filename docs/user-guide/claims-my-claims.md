# Claim back money you spent for the company

## What it is

**My claims** is where you ask the company to pay back what you spent for it: a receipt, or a
trip in your own car. If claims are all you do in Fyniq, Fyniq opens on My claims and shows only
your own items; you never see the company's figures.

## Before you start

- Have the receipt ready: a photo, a screenshot, a PDF, or the email it came in. You can also
  forward a receipt email to your own capture address and pick it up later.
- Read the company's expense policy under **Before you claim**. It names the limits per
  category, the receipts it needs and the deadline for claiming.
- Using Nemura? Open **Claims** on your Nemura home. It opens My claims with the same sign-in,
  and **Back to People** takes you back.

## Steps

1. Open **My claims**.
2. Select **New claim from a receipt**. Paste the receipt, drag it in, or use **Camera** on a
   phone.
3. Check what Fyniq read from the receipt: the date, the amount and currency, the category and,
   if you worked for a project, the project. Split the claim into lines if one receipt covers
   different things.
4. For a trip in your own car, select **Mileage trip** instead: the date, the purpose, where you
   went from and to, and the distance. The amount is the distance times the company's rate for
   your vehicle type.
5. Fix anything the policy check flags, then submit the claim.
6. Follow it under **Your claims**: draft, submitted, approved, scheduled, paid or returned.
   The page also shows what the company owes you in total.

## What happens next

- Your claim goes to your line manager first, then to finance. In Standard mode, nobody approves
  their own claim.
- Once approved, it is paid in the next payment batch, or with your salary as a reimbursement.
  A reimbursement of what you actually spent for work is not part of your pay.
- A claim in another currency is paid back in Singapore dollars.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The receipt was already claimed or captured | The claim is blocked. If it is not a duplicate, a person in finance records why, and then it can go ahead |
| The claim is above the category's limit | It can still be sent; the extra approver the policy names checks it |
| The claim is past the policy's deadline | Finance must approve it, with a reason |
| The claim came back as returned | Read the approver's note, fix the claim and submit it again |
