# Your home page in Nemura

## What it is

- Nemura opens the home page that suits the person's roles after sign-in: **My home** for everyone but the people team, **People home** for the people team.
- Neither shows the company's finances. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#what-it-is)

## What to do

1. Sign in first: [[nemura.sign-in]].
2. Read **Your roles**; they decide what the person can see and do.
3. With no role yet, ask the Owner for one.
4. Leave ([[nemura.my-leave]]), pay and letters appear as the company switches each on.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#steps)

## My home

- **My home** shows tiles for what a person does most: **Apply for leave**, **Ask for a letter**, **Update my details**, **Ask a question**, **My leave** and **My requests**. See [[nemura.my-details]] and [[nemura.ask-hr]].
- **At a glance** shows annual leave left, the next public holiday and how many requests are open; a figure not switched on or not allowed is left out, never shown as zero. Payslip, next pay day and Claims appear when payroll and the Fyniq link are switched on. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#what-it-is)

## Today (People home)

- The people team's home shows leave requests waiting, who is away today, certificates to verify, flexible work requests waiting, government-paid leave with no claim filed and what needs a look; each links to its page, and a figure the person may not see shows a dash. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#what-it-is)

## Related

- After a person's last day the home page is **My documents**; see [[nemura.leaver-account]].
- Find colleagues in [[nemura.people]]; the HR administrator and the Owner switch features in [[nemura.features]]; the country pack and leave policy are in [[nemura.packs]]. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#what-it-is)

## When it looks wrong

- **There is nothing at this address**: select **Go to my home**.
- A wrong role: only the Owner changes roles. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#common-problems)
