Which data may go where

  • Know-how Guide pages, policies, the knowledge base. Any connection you turn on. 4 connections
  • Business figures Balances, invoices, bills and reports. Claude and Gemini on Agent Platform, Singapore. 2 connections
  • Personal data Names in bank lines, receipts and contacts. Only processing in Singapore or a model you host in your own Singapore network. 2 connections
  • Payroll and restricted Salaries and pay runs. Claude on Agent Platform, Singapore, and only while AI payroll access is on. 1 connection
The exclusions in AI settings apply to every provider: NRIC and FIN numbers, full bank account numbers and secrets never leave Fyniq. A route can never send an agent's data to a connection this policy does not allow.

How a connection is paid for

  • Usage-basedCharged per token. Fyniq works out each request's cost from the provider's own usage figures and price table.
  • Subscription or commitmentA fixed fee with a quota, such as reserved throughput or prepaid credits. When the quota runs out, the agent moves to its next route step or stops; it never switches to pay-per-token on its own.
  • A person's own planWhere a provider lets subscribers use their plan in other apps. Only for that person's own chats; arrives in phase 2. Off
Not supported Signing in with a consumer chat plan whose terms keep it for the provider's own apps, such as Claude Free, Pro or Max, and reusing a chat session or browser cookie.

Two people for every change

Adding, changing or turning on a connection, and allowing one of the models its provider lists, needs you and a second Owner. A model serves no agent until it passes that agent's evals.

Model lists

Fyniq has no built-in list of models. Each connection's models come from its provider, read with that connection's own key or grant: every day at 06:00, when a key is stored, and when you refresh. No company data is sent.
