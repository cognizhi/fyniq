# Erase a former employee's data

## What it is

When a former employee's records are no longer needed, an Owner or an HR administrator can have their personal data erased, and a second Owner or HR administrator approves it. It is allowed only after the retention periods have run: one year after the person left for the employment records, and, for someone paid through Nemura, five years from the relevant Year of Assessment for the payroll records (the accountant confirms the exact reach for IR8A and CPF records).

## Ask for an erasure

1. Open **Erasures** and enter the person's ID and why it is asked for.
2. Select **Ask for the erasure** and confirm with your passkey.
3. A second Owner or HR administrator, not you, selects **Approve** and confirms with their passkey. Nemura then erases and keeps a signed record outside its database.

It is refused, with the reason, when the person has not left, the periods have not run (the page names the date), a legal hold keeps their records, or they have an access request that is not answered yet.

## What is erased, and what stays

The person's names, work and personal email, phone, address, emergency contacts, NRIC or FIN, salary account and reason for leaving are emptied, their sign-in is switched off, their files (payslip and letter PDFs, documents) are deleted, and Fyniq is told to clear the name and email it held. The itemised figures of payslips made from now on are sealed under a key of the person's own, and erasing destroys the key, so they can never be read again. Other records the database keeps append-only (issued letters, older payslip figures, pay run lines, leave and sick notes) stay, tied to the person's ID with no name; the people team decides how those are shredded. After a restore of Nemura's database, the signed record is applied again before it opens, so nothing erased comes back.

## Records due for deletion

Each night Nemura lists the records whose keeping period has run: grievances five years after the outcome, and candidates who are not progressing six months after the decision unless they consented to be kept. Under **Records due for deletion** on the Erasures page the Owner or an HR administrator sees, for each kind, how many are due and how many a legal hold keeps, and the day the list must have been reviewed (30 days). Select **I have reviewed the list** once you have seen it; the next nightly job then deletes what is still due and not held and keeps a proof that holds no name and no word. A list not reviewed by its date is marked **Not reviewed by its date** here. No person is named on this page.

## Common problems

- **The person has not left.** Record their leaving date first.
- **The records must be kept longer.** Ask again on the date the message gives.
- **A legal hold keeps the records.** Release the hold, or leave the records as they are.
- **A second person approves.** You asked for it, so another Owner or HR administrator approves it.
