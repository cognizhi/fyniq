# Ask the assistant about your books

## What it is

- **Assistant** in the header, or **Ctrl+J** (**⌘J** on a Mac), opens the assistant beside the page you are on. On a
  wide screen it sits beside the page; on a tablet it opens over the page; on a phone it fills the screen.
- Ask in your own words, such as "What was our revenue last month?". The assistant reads the books as you: it sees only
  what you may see, and it changes nothing.
- Everything the assistant writes is marked **AI** and shown in violet, so you can tell it from what people entered.

## Where each figure comes from

- Fyniq writes every figure in an answer from the books, never the assistant. Each figure shows as a highlighted
  value, and beneath the answer a chip names where it came from, such as a report and its months. Select the chip to
  open that report, ledger or record.
- When a figure cannot be established, the answer says so instead of guessing. If Fyniq could not check a figure
  against the books, it leaves the figure out and says how many it left out.
- In Expert mode, each answer also lists what each figure was read from and the ledger revision it was read at.

## What it never sees

- Identity numbers (NRIC and FIN), bank account numbers, secrets and home addresses never reach the assistant, even if
  you type one into a question.
- Payroll reaches it only if an Owner turns on AI payroll access and you may see payroll yourself.

## Your conversations

- Each conversation is yours alone. **Your conversations** lists them, newest first; select one to carry on.
  **New conversation** starts afresh.
- **Delete** removes a conversation from your list at once, and Fyniq erases it for good overnight. Conversations you
  keep are erased once the organisation's retention for them ends.
- A conversation holds a limited number of questions; when it is full, start a new one.

## When it does not answer

- **The assistant is switched off**: the Owner has switched the AI off, or has not switched it on yet. Nothing you
  type is sent anywhere.
- **The AI budget is spent for this month**: the question was not sent. An Owner can raise the budget in AI settings.
- Too many questions in a minute or a day: the panel says so, and when you can ask again.
