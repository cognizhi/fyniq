# Set your goals and update their key results

## What it is

- My goals shows the company's and the teams' goals, your own goals with key results and the KPIs your manager set. A key result moves from a start value to a target; progress is exact and rounded down, and the status says whether it keeps up with the time that has passed. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/goals.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Add a goal with its due date, aligned with a company or team goal if there is one, and a key result with its unit and target (below the start value when lower is better).
2. Update a key result with the value now; the history keeps who gave it. Mark the goal done once.
3. Your manager or the HR administrator can set your KPIs and goals for you; you update your own KPI. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/goals.md#steps)

## Who reads what

- Everyone reads the company's and the teams' goals; only you, your line manager and the people team read your own goals and KPIs. Nemura shows no company financial figure. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/goals.md#before-you-start)

## When it looks wrong

- "No such goal or person for you" means you may not read or change it. A value is a decimal number with at most four decimals; a target sits on the side of the start value that matches the direction. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/goals.md#common-problems)
