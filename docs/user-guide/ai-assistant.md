# Ask the assistant about your books

## What it is

- **Assistant** in the header, or **Ctrl+J** (**⌘J** on a Mac), opens the assistant beside the page you are on. On a
  wide screen it sits beside the page; on a tablet it opens over the page; on a phone it fills the screen.
- Ask in your own words, such as "What was our revenue last month?". The assistant reads the books as you: it sees only
  what you may see, and it changes nothing.
- Everything the assistant writes is marked **AI** and shown in violet, so you can tell it from what people entered.

## Ask about a record, a report or a figure

- **Ask about this** on an invoice, a bill, a journal, a contact or a report, or on a figure you are tracing, opens
  the assistant with it as the context: the chip at the top says what you are asking about, and **Remove** takes it
  off. It is not shown while the AI is switched off.

## Explain and simulate

- **Ask** takes a question in your own words.
- **Explain** asks the assistant where what you are asking about comes from and what it is made of: the report a
  figure is read from and the accounts behind it, or what a record holds.
- **Simulate** works on an invoice or a bill you asked about. It shows the lowest cash balance in the next 90 days,
  and the first day below your buffer, as they stand and as they would be if it were paid on another day or not in
  those 90 days. These figures are Fyniq's, not the assistant's, and nothing is saved or changed. **Cash ahead** has
  more what-ifs.

## Where each figure comes from

- Fyniq writes every figure in an answer from the books, never the assistant. Each figure shows as a highlighted
  value, and beneath the answer a chip names where it came from, such as a report and its months. Select the chip to
  open that report, ledger or record.
- When a figure cannot be established, the answer says so instead of guessing. If Fyniq could not check a figure
  against the books, it leaves the figure out and says how many it left out.
- In Expert mode, each answer also lists what each figure was read from and the ledger revision it was read at.

## Ask how something works

- Ask how Fyniq works, what a status means, or what your organisation decided, such as "How soon must a claim be
  submitted?". The assistant answers from Fyniq's guide, in its own words, and never from memory.
- Beneath the answer, a chip names the guide page each explanation comes from. Select it to open that page. The page is
  one you can open in the user guide; the assistant's own working notes are never shown or linked.
- A chip marked **awaiting review** points to a guide page that has not been reviewed yet; the assistant says so when it
  relies on it.
- The guide explains how things work. It holds no figures of your books: those always come from the books, as above. For
  a tax rate, threshold or deadline the assistant checks Fyniq's statutory values and says so when the guide may be out
  of date.
- It only draws on guide pages you may open, so it never explains something your role cannot see.

## What it never sees

- Identity numbers (NRIC and FIN), bank account numbers, secrets and home addresses never reach the assistant, even if
  you type one into a question.
- Payroll reaches it only if an Owner turns on AI payroll access and you may see payroll yourself.

## Rate an answer

- Under each answer, the thumbs up or down rates it, with a reason in your words. You rate an answer once.
- An Owner or Administrator reviews each rating with your question and the answer. Only what they add to the golden
  set is used to test the assistant; see the AI activity page.

## Your conversations

- Each conversation is yours alone. **Your conversations** lists them, newest first; select one to carry on.
  **New conversation** starts afresh.
- **Delete** removes a conversation from your list at once, and Fyniq erases it for good overnight. Conversations you
  keep are erased once the organisation's retention for them ends.
- A conversation holds a limited number of questions; when it is full, start a new one.

## When it does not answer

- **The assistant is switched off**: the Owner has switched the AI off, or has not switched it on yet. Nothing you
  type is sent anywhere.
- **The AI budget is spent for this month**: the question was not sent. An Owner can raise the budget in AI settings.
- Too many questions in a minute or a day: the panel says so, and when you can ask again.
