# Privacy notice for our people

This notice tells you, as someone who works with or for the company and uses its finance system, what personal data
the company keeps about you, why, and what you can ask. Questions go to our Data Protection Officer, whose contact is
at the foot of this notice and of every email the system sends.

## What we keep

- Your name, work email address and the roles you hold, so you can sign in and do your work.
- When you sign in and from where (your IP address and browser), kept 400 days for security, then erased.
- What you do in the system: each change you save is in the audit trail, with your name, so the books can be trusted.
- If you claim expenses: your claims, receipts and the bank account they are repaid to. Your bank account number is
  encrypted and shown masked.
- If you are a director or shareholder: what the company owes you or you owe it, and your holdings.

We do not keep your NRIC or FIN number in the finance system.

## Why

[counsel to confirm] To manage your work with the company: to let you sign in, to keep accurate and auditable books as
the law requires, to repay your expenses, and to keep the system secure. The company does not need your consent for
these purposes, but it tells you about them here.

## Who sees it

People in the company see what their roles allow; an auditor or tax agent the company engages sees what they need. The
system's providers process it for us under contract, in Singapore.

## How long we keep it

- The books and their audit trail: at least 7 years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as the
  Companies Act requires. After that, the personal details in the audit trail are destroyed.
- Sign-in details: 400 days.
- Your account: while you work with the company; after you leave, only what the records need.

## What you can ask

[counsel to confirm] You can ask to see the personal data the company holds about you, and to correct it. Ask in
Settings, under My personal data, or write to the Data Protection Officer. We answer within 30 days, or tell you in
writing within 30 days when we will.

## Changes

When this notice changes, the system asks you to read the new version.
