# Tell us you're sick

## What it is

Sick leave is not applied for: you tell the company you are away, with **Tell us you're sick**. Nobody has to approve it.
The people team verifies the medical certificate, and only the HR officer and the HR administrator see it. Your manager sees
only that you are **Away**.

## Before you start

- Outpatient sick leave and hospitalisation leave share one limit: the outpatient days count towards the hospitalisation days.
- In Singapore, under the Employment Act, an employee gets paid sick leave after 3 months' service: 5 days outpatient and 15
  hospitalisation at 3 months, 8 and 30 at 4 months, 11 and 45 at 5 months, and 14 and 60 from 6 months. The 60 include the
  14. Nemura applies the limit in force on each day you were away. Your company may give more.
- The sick leave must be certified by a registered medical practitioner. These values come from MOM's page, read for the
  country pack, and have not yet been checked by a second person.

## Steps

1. Open **Tell us you're sick**.
2. Choose **Outpatient** or **Hospitalisation**.
3. Give the **First day** and the **Last day** (the same day for one day), and a note if you want to.
4. Select **Tell the company**. Nemura counts your working days, takes the paid days from your sick leave limit and shows how
   many were paid and how many were beyond it.
5. When you are back, give the certificate under **Give the medical certificate**: the clinic or doctor on it, and a photo or
   the e-MC file.

## The 48-hour rule

Tell your employer, or try to, within 48 hours of being away, and give the medical certificate when you return. Nemura accepts
a notice that comes later and marks it late, so the people team can see it.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Some days are shown as beyond the limit | Those days are not paid sick leave. The people team decides how they are handled, for example as annual leave or unpaid leave |
| Nothing is paid, you started less than 3 months ago | Paid sick leave starts after 3 months' service. Ask the people team how your company handles the days before that |
| You already have leave or sick leave on some of those days | Ask the people team to correct it, or choose the days again |
| Those days are rest days or public holidays | There is nothing to record |
| A certificate is already given | It is given once. Ask the people team if it is wrong |
