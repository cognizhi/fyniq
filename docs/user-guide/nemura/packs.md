# Read the country pack and set your leave policy against it

## What it is

The **Country pack** page lists the statutory facts Nemura uses for Singapore: leave days, public holidays, when salary is
paid, how long employment records are kept and the retirement ages. Each value comes from an official page, which the
pack names with the day it was read and what it said. A value that is **researched** has been read from the official page
but not yet checked by a second person. Your **Leave policy** sits on top of the statute.

## Before you start

- You need to be the HR administrator or the Owner to accept a version, record a decision or set the policy; the HR
  officer, the Payroll administrator and the Administrator can read them.
- Accepting a version verifies nothing. The pack's publisher verifies the values.

## Steps

1. Open **Country pack**. Read the sources of each version.
2. Under **Values to decide on**, give your company's reason for using each researched value, then select **Record decision**. A calculation that needs a researched value has no result until you decide.
3. Open **Leave policy**. Enter the day it starts, the annual leave days by year of service, the sick leave days at 3, 4, 5 and 6 months and the childcare leave days, then select **Set policy**.

## Common problems

- **The policy gives less than the statute.** Nemura names each rule and its statutory days. Raise each to at least the statute.
- **A policy that gives more** is accepted, with a note saying so.
- **What needs a look** lists a work pass about to expire, next year's public holidays missing from the pack from 1 December, and researched values your policy uses with no decision.
- **A worksite outside Singapore is refused.** This release has the Singapore pack only.
