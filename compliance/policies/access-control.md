# Access control policy

## Purpose

This policy says who may reach what, so that each person can do their job and nothing more, and no one person can both
make and approve a payment.

## Scope

Fyniq, the company's bank accounts, email, cloud services and every system that holds company or personal data.

## What we do

- **Least privilege.** Each person gets the roles their job needs; access to personal data is limited to those who
  need it to do their work.
- **Second factor.** Every account uses a second factor.
- **Separation of duties.** Whoever prepares a payment, or a change of someone's bank details, does not approve it.
- **Joiners, movers and leavers.** Access is given when someone joins, changed when their job changes, and removed
  the day they leave. People from outside the company get an end date.
- **Review.** Each quarter an Owner reviews who can do what, and signs off the review.

## What Fyniq enforces

- Every member signs in with a second factor (CTL-IAM-01), and the quarterly access review is tracked until it is
  signed off (CTL-IAM-02).
- Roles grant only what Appendix D allows, for every operation (CTL-IAM-06), and conflicts of duties are found each
  night (CTL-FIN-07).
- Payments and bank detail changes need a second person (CTL-FIN-08).

## Roles

The Administrator manages members and roles; an Owner signs off the quarterly review. The Owner reviews this policy at
least once a year.
