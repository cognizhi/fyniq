confirms the statement; a secondary source never lifts one. This is general information, not legal or
tax advice: the accountant, the tax agent and the DPO still decide the items marked as theirs.

stays on the unconfirmed part), **Not confirmed** (no primary source found; the tag stays),
**Professional judgement** (research cannot settle it), **Checked at build** (a vendor or build-time
check that stays a P0/P1 task).
