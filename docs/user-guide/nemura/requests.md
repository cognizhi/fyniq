# See your requests and make a flexible work request

## What it is

**My requests** shows where your leave requests and your flexible work requests are. A **flexible work request** is a formal
request to change when, where or how much you work. Under the Tripartite Guidelines on Flexible Work Arrangement Requests,
which took effect on 1 December 2024, the company has 2 months to respond. Nemura dates your request the day you send it and
shows the day the written reply is due. A clarification or a change of type does not restart that date.

## Before you start

- Sign in first: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).
- Leave must be switched on for your organisation.

## Steps

1. Open **My requests**. Under **Leave**, you can **Cancel** a request that is waiting, or an approved one before it starts.
2. Under **Flexible work**, read each request and, once the company has answered, its written reply.
3. To make a request, fill in **Make a formal flexible work request**: what you want to change, the day it would start, the day
   it would end (leave it empty if open) and why. Select **Send the request**.
4. You can **Withdraw** a request while it waits for a reply.

## What happens next

- The HR officer, the HR administrator or the Owner answers in writing: accepted, refused, or an alternative. A refusal or an
  alternative gives its reason. The answer is kept as the record. Nobody answers their own request.
- If no written reply has been given as the 2 months run out, **What needs a look** shows it to the people team.

## Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Leave is switched off for your organisation | The Owner or the HR administrator switches it on under Features |
| Your login is linked to no person record | Ask the people team to link it |
| The reply is later than the date shown | Ask the people team; the date is the guidelines' limit, and they are warned |
