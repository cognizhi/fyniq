Results are kept per model version and route. None of your routes uses a moving model name; one that does is re-qualified every week and shows a warning until it passes. A connection can also test the models its provider newly lists, by itself; putting one on a route stays your decision.

When a step fails

  1. 1An error, a time-out, a rate limit or a used-up quota moves the request to the next step.
  2. 2Only if that step may receive the agent's data and has the capabilities it needs.
  3. 3Otherwise the run stops with a plain message and an error code. Protection is never lowered to keep going.

Every fallback is on the AI activity log.

Who changes a route

An Owner or the Administrator. A change that sends an agent's data somewhere new, such as another country, needs the Owner and a second approver.
