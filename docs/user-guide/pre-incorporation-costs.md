# Costs before the books start

## What it is

Accounting › **Costs before incorporation** keeps the costs a director or shareholder paid
themselves before the company's books start: the incorporation fees, a lawyer, the first
subscriptions. Each keeps the date on its receipt. None of them posts until your accountant
decides how it is treated. Once decided, the cost is owed back to the person who paid it, on
their director's account (2500 Loan from director / shareholder), as a cost they paid
personally.

This page is general information, not tax advice. Your accountant decides each cost.

## Record a cost

1. Select **Add a cost**. An Owner or the Controller records it.
2. Enter the date on the receipt, which is before the books start, who it was paid to, what it
   was for, the director or shareholder who paid it, the amount they paid and any GST the
   receipt shows.
3. Attach the receipt or invoice. The accountant cannot decide a cost without one.

The cost then shows **Needs decision**. Until the accountant decides it, you can still change it
or take its file off again.

## What the accountant decides

For each cost the accountant chooses:

- **The income-tax treatment:** deductible (a revenue cost from up to 12 months before the
  business started), capital (such as incorporation costs or equipment), or not deductible.
  Founders' own costs from before incorporation are for the tax agent to confirm.
- **The account** it posts to.
- **Whether its GST is claimed.** Only while the company is GST-registered. GST paid before
  registration is claimed in the first GST return only where IRAS's rules allow it, such as
  services within 6 months before registration or goods still held; otherwise the GST stays in
  the cost.

The person who paid a cost never decides it, even when they are also the accountant.

## When it posts

The decision posts the cost at once: the account the accountant chose is debited, and the
person's director's account on 2500 is credited with what they paid. It posts on the books
start, or on the day of the decision when the books start's month is already closed.

A decided cost changes no more. To correct one, post a journal to the person's director's
account with the reason.

## What the page shows

- Every cost, with its date, who paid it, the amount and the accountant's decision, filtered by
  **Needs decision** or **Decided**.
- **Owed to the founders:** for each person, what their costs come to, already decided and still
  waiting for the accountant.

## Common problems

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| The cost is not before the books start | Record a cost from the books start on as an expense claim or a bill instead |
| The organisation has no books start | Set the books start in Settings › Organisation first |
| The person is not a director or shareholder | Mark the contact as a director or shareholder in Contacts, or choose another |
| The cost has no receipt attached | Attach the receipt or invoice the person paid, then decide it |
| You paid this cost, so another person decides it | Ask the accountant to decide it |
| The organisation was not GST-registered on the posting date | Leave the GST in the cost |
| The cost is decided and posted, so it changes no more | Correct it with a journal to the person's director's account |
