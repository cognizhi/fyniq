# Your home page in Nemura

## What it is

- Nemura opens the home page that suits the person's roles after sign-in: **My home** for everyone but the people team, **People home** for the people team.
- Neither shows the company's finances. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#what-it-is)

## What to do

1. Sign in first: [[nemura.sign-in]].
2. Read **Your roles**; they decide what the person can see and do.
3. With no role yet, ask the Owner for one.
4. Leave, pay and letters appear as the company switches each on.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#steps)

## When it looks wrong

- **There is nothing at this address**: select **Go to my home**.
- A wrong role: only the Owner changes roles. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/home.md#common-problems)
