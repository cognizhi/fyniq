# Read the board packs and investor updates

## Find it

Sign in and open the **Board and investor portal**. It is where a Board viewer or an investor reads the
numbers.

## What you see

- **Investor updates** sent to you. A new one is marked **New**; opening it records that you have read it.
- **Board packs** that have been published, with their figures as they were frozen when they were published.
- **My holding**, if you sign in as a shareholder: the shares you hold by class.

You never see a draft pack, an update that was not sent to you, or anyone else's holding. An email or
notification that tells you something is ready carries no figure: the figures are here, after you sign in.

## What you do not see

The full cap table opens only when an Owner grants it to you.
