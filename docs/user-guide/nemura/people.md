# Find a colleague in the people directory

## What it is

The **People** page lists everyone who works here today: name, job title, department, manager and work email.
Everyone who signs in can read it. It never shows anything restricted, such as an identity number, a bank
account, pay or a date of birth.

## Before you start

- Sign in to Nemura: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).

## Steps

1. Open **People** from the top of any page.
2. Type a name, a job title, a department or a manager under **Find a person**.
3. The people team (HR, the Payroll administrator and the Owner) can select a name to open the person's record.

## Common problems

- **Nobody works here yet.** The HR team adds people; ask them.
- **A colleague is missing.** The page shows who works here today: someone who has not started yet, or has left, is not listed.
- **A name is not a link.** Only the people team opens a record; you can read your own from your home page.
