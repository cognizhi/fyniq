# Approve a change

## What it is

Some changes are too important for one person to make alone: settings such as thresholds,
retention and the AI assistant, activating a statutory pack version, and, once the company has a
second member, adding or ending a role and inviting someone. Fyniq asks for approval instead of making the change. Another person
who approves that kind of change, such as an Owner, then approves or rejects it. Nobody approves
their own request.

## Ask for a change

1. Make the change as usual, for example on **Settings → Organisation** or **Settings → Members**.
2. Confirm it's you with your passkey or authenticator app when Fyniq asks.
3. Fyniq shows **Waiting for approval**. Nothing has changed yet.

Only one request waits for each setting or person at a time.

## Approve or reject

1. Open **Inbox → To approve** and choose the request.
2. Check what it changes: each value before and after.
3. Select **Approve…** or **Reject…**. The confirmation shows exactly what you sign, your step, and
   the request's fingerprint. To reject, say why.
4. Select **Approve with passkey** (or **Reject with passkey**) and confirm on your device. Without
   a passkey, select **Approve** or **Reject** and confirm it's you when Fyniq asks.

The Inbox never approves anything by itself: every decision goes through the confirmation.

Your approval signs exactly what you were shown. Fyniq records who approved, when, and how they
confirmed it was them, in the audit trail.

With a passkey, your device signs the request itself: the confirmation carries a fingerprint of
exactly what you approve or reject, and works once, for that request, within 5 minutes. Fyniq keeps
your device's signature with the approval, so anyone checking later can verify it.

Some changes need more than one approval, in steps ([Choose who approves a change](approval-policies.md)).
The request shows each step and the approvals it has. The change is made with the last approval.
You approve a request once, and only at a step that names you or one of your roles.

## Withdraw your request

1. Open the request you asked for, from **Inbox → Your requests, waiting**.
2. Select **Withdraw…**, then **Withdraw**.

The request is cancelled with any approvals it had, and nothing changes. Only the person who asked
can withdraw a request; anyone else who disagrees rejects it with a reason.

## Review high-risk actions

Every high-risk change is logged when it is made: each change another person approved, and each role
or invitation the Owner set up alone before a second person joined. Once a week, another founder
reviews the log.

1. Open **Inbox → Weekly review**. The **Review log** lists the actions nobody has reviewed yet,
   with who did each.
2. Tick the ones you checked and select **Mark as reviewed**. Add a note if you like, then select
   **Mark as reviewed** again.

You can't review an action you took yourself. Each action is reviewed once. An action still
unreviewed 14 days after it was made shows as overdue in the Inbox and the review log.

## When a request can no longer be approved

- **Changed**: if the setting, the person's roles, the approval policy or the team mode changed
  after the request, the request is void and nothing changes. Ask for the change again.
- **An approver lost their right**: if someone who approved an earlier step no longer has the
  role their step needed when the last approval comes, nothing changes and the request is void.
- **Expired**: a request waits 7 days. After that, ask for the change again.
- **Rejected**: the change is not made. The reason is shown on the request.
