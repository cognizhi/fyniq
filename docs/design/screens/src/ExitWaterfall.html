Amounts in SGD. Preference is what an investor gets by keeping it; as converted is its share of a plain split.

Each investor takes the larger of its preference and its share of a split by shares. At S$8,000,000 every investor is better off with its preference, so each founder receives S$225,525.02 less than a plain split.

  • Above thatEveryone converts: a plain split by shares
