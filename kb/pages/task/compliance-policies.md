# Read, acknowledge and publish the policies

## What it is

- The policy library holds eight policies: information security, acceptable use, access control, incident response, backup, change management, AI use and data protection.
- Each policy has a version in force, which every member reads and accepts for themselves.
- It sits under **Compliance** › **Risks and policies**, below the risk register ([[compliance.risks]]), with each policy's version in force, when it was published and how many members acknowledged it.
- The same library holds the two privacy notices, listed on **Compliance** › **Privacy notices and consent**; the members' notice is acknowledged as a policy is ([[compliance.privacy-notices]]).

(src: docs/user-guide/compliance-policies.md#what-it-is)

## Acknowledge a policy

- Every member acknowledges a published policy, or a new version of it, within 30 days: 30 days after it was published, or after they joined if that was later.
- The **Acknowledge policy** card on **Home** and in the **Inbox** lists the policies waiting, each with its due day; it shows **Overdue** once that day has passed.
- Select **Read**, read the version in force, then **I have read and accept it**; **Later** closes it without recording anything.
- The same list is at the top of **Compliance** › **Reviews and attestations**, beside the other recurring reviews ([[compliance.reviews]]).
- Each version is acknowledged once, signed in as yourself; an integration or the AI assistant cannot acknowledge for you. A new version asks again.

(src: docs/user-guide/compliance-policies.md#acknowledge-a-policy)

## Draft and publish a policy

- The Owner and the Compliance officer keep the library ([[members.roles]]).
- Select **Draft**: until someone edits it, the draft is Fyniq's starting draft, written from official sources such as the PDPC's guides.
- Adopt it as it is, or edit it and select **Save the draft**; each save is a new revision and the earlier ones are kept. **Publish version** makes the draft the next version.
- A published version never changes; a draft the same as the version in force cannot be published again. Clauses marked [counsel to confirm] bear on the law: have counsel confirm them before publishing.
- Every draft, version and acknowledgement is kept and is in the audit trail ([[compliance.audit-trail]]).

(src: docs/user-guide/compliance-policies.md#draft-and-publish-a-policy)
