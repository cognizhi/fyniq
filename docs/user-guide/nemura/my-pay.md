# Read your payslips

## What it is

**My pay** lists your payslips by month, each with the net salary you were paid and the day you were paid. A payslip holds the twelve items MOM requires of an itemised pay slip: the employer's and
your full names, the date of payment, your basic salary, the start and end of the salary period, your allowances, any other additional payments such as a bonus, your deductions (your CPF contribution,
a self-help group fund, no-pay leave and any other), your overtime hours, overtime pay and overtime payment period when overtime applies, and your net salary. Only you read your payslips.

## Before you start

- Sign in first: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md). Your login must be linked to your person record.
- A payslip appears once the people who run payroll have approved the run and published its payslips, when your salary is paid or within three working days of payment.

## Steps

1. Open **My pay** and select a month to read the payslip.
2. Select **Print** to print it, or **Open the PDF** to save it. The PDF is the exact document that was issued: if its stored bytes ever stopped matching its hash, Nemura would refuse to show it.
3. If a payslip is marked **Corrected**, a newer payslip replaces it: read the newer one.

## What happens next

- If your pay is corrected, you get a corrected payslip for that month; the first stays, marked as corrected.
- After you leave, your narrow account keeps your payslips for the record-keeping period: see [Use your account after you leave](leaver-account.md).

## Common problems

- **There is no payslip for this month.** The run is not approved or its payslips are not published yet: ask the people who run payroll.
- **A figure looks wrong.** Ask the people team a question: see [Ask the people team a question](ask-hr.md). A correction is a new version of the run and a new payslip.
