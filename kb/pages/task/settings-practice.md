# Try a task in a practice organisation

## What it is

- A separate organisation labelled **Practice**, to try a task without touching your real books; it starts with empty books and nothing copied from your company.
- Only an Owner of an organisation opens one, and only one at a time.

(src: docs/user-guide/practice-organisation.md#what-it-is)

## Open one

1. **Settings → Organisation**, then **Practice organisation**.
2. **Open a practice organisation** (confirming it's you if asked).
3. It appears under **Your organisations**: select **Switch** beside it.

- Every page in it shows **Practice organisation** at the top; select **Back to** your company there, or **Switch** under **Settings → Organisation**, to return.

(src: docs/user-guide/practice-organisation.md#open-one)

## Good to know

- You are the Owner of your practice organisation and nobody else is a member; it does not see your company's data and your company does not see its.
- You hold one at a time, and cannot open one from a practice organisation; opening one is recorded in both organisations' audit trails.
- Fyniq has no way yet to delete an organisation, so a practice organisation stays until one is built.

(src: docs/user-guide/practice-organisation.md#good-to-know)
