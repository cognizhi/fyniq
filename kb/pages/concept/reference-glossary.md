# Glossary

## What it is

- The words Fyniq uses for money and the books, in plain words; on a page, a glossary word is underlined with dots and shows its meaning on hover, focus or a tap.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#glossary)

## Accounts payable

- What the company owes its suppliers for bills it has not paid yet. Bills to pay are listed under **Purchases**.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#accounts-payable)

## Accounts receivable

- What customers owe the company for invoices they have not paid yet. Each customer's statement shows theirs.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#accounts-receivable)

## Accrual

- Recording income or a cost in the month it belongs to, even when the money moves in another month. For example, December's electricity, billed in January, is a December cost.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#accrual)

## Balance sheet

- What the company has and what it owes at a date, and the owners' share: what is left when what it owes is taken from what it has.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#balance-sheet)

## Close

- Checking a month's books and then locking the month, so nothing changes it. A closed month takes no new entry; changing it means reopening it, which another person approves.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#close)

## Credit note

- A document that takes an amount off an invoice: off what a customer owes, or, from a supplier, off what the company owes them.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#credit-note)

## Debit and credit

- The two sides of every entry in the books. Every entry's debits equal its credits, so the books always balance. Money coming into a bank account is a debit to that account; the account it is for, such as sales, takes the credit.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#debit-and-credit)

## Deferred revenue

- Money billed for a service the company has not provided yet. It becomes revenue month by month, as the service is provided.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#deferred-revenue)

## Dimension

- A tag on a line, such as a department or a project, so its revenue or cost can be reported by it.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#dimension)

## GST

- Singapore's Goods and Services Tax. A GST-registered company charges it on what it sells and claims back what it paid on what it buys; the difference goes to IRAS with each GST return.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#gst)

## Journal

- An entry made directly in the books, as debits and credits, for something no invoice, bill or bank line records, such as a correction or a year-end adjustment.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#journal)

## Ledger

- The record of every entry the books hold, account by account.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#ledger)

## Net burn

- The cash a month's operations and investments used, less the cash they brought in.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#net-burn)

## Posting

- Writing an entry into the ledger. A posted entry never changes: a mistake is corrected by a reversal, so the history shows both.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#posting)

## Prepayment

- A cost paid in advance, such as a year's insurance, which becomes a cost month by month as the company uses what it paid for.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#prepayment)

## Reconciliation

- Checking that the bank's lines and the books agree, line by line, so every amount in the bank is in the books once.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#reconciliation)

## Reversal

- An entry that undoes a posted entry exactly. Both stay in the books, so you can see what changed and why.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#reversal)

## Runway

- How many months the cash in the bank would last at the average net burn of the last three months.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#runway)

## Suspense

- The account (2999) where money waits until someone says what it is for. It should be empty when a month is closed.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#suspense)

## Trial balance

- Every account's balance at a date. Its debits equal its credits.

(src: docs/user-guide/glossary.md#trial-balance)
