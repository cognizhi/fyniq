# Plan a joiner's first weeks, invite them to Fyniq and record who is leaving

## What it is

**Joiners and leavers** has three parts. **Onboarding plans** give each new person a list of tasks for HR, their manager, IT and themselves, each due a number of days from their start date (the key employment terms within 14 days, bank and CPF details before the first pay run, pass collection, equipment, accounts, introductions). The next part is the invitation to Fyniq, and the last the leavers' dates. When your company uses Fyniq, adding a person raises a task for whoever may invite people in Fyniq: invite them as an employee, so
they can make claims, and mark the task done. Nemura never grants a Fyniq role itself. For a person who is leaving, it keeps the dates that start statutory clocks apart, a
checklist, and the annual leave they are left with on their last day.

## Before you start

- To invite people to Fyniq, you are the HR administrator, the Administrator or the Owner.
- To record a leaving, you are the HR officer, the HR administrator or the Owner.

## Steps

1. Under **Invite new people to Fyniq**, the HR administrator or the Owner selects **Raise a task for each new person** once the company uses Fyniq. It raises no task for people
   already added.
2. Invite each person in Fyniq with the Employee role, then select **Mark the person invited**. If the person will not claim expenses, select **Dismiss the task**.
3. Under **Record a leaving**, choose the person and how the employment ends. Enter the day notice was given or the company learned of the cessation, and the **last day of
   employment**, the notice period included. For a retrenchment also enter the day employees were told. Tick off the checklist dates as they happen: equipment returned, exit
   interview, Fyniq access removed. Select **Save the leaving**.
4. Ask for the person's service letter under their record when they leave: see [Ask for a letter](letters-request.md).

## Plan a joiner's first weeks

1. Switch Onboarding on under **Settings, Features** (it needs Letters).
2. Under **Onboarding plans**, select **Make the template** to copy the standard one under a name of your own, such as Office joiner. A template is never edited: make a new one and archive the old, and plans already made keep their tasks.
3. Choose the person and the template and select **Start the plan**. Each task is due from the person's start date; a person has one plan.
4. Each person sees the tasks that are theirs on **My home** under **Your onboarding tasks**: the joiner their own, a line manager those of their reports, IT (the Administrator, the HR administrator or the Owner) IT's, and the people team HR's. Select **Done** when it is done. A task is decided once; only the people team may skip one that does not apply, and they may mark any task done, except a task that closes by itself.
5. Two tasks close by themselves, in the same step as the work: **Issue the key employment terms (KETs)** closes when the person's KETs letter is issued (whoever signs it), and a task that names the invitation closes when the person is marked as invited to Fyniq. They show **Closes by itself** instead of a Done button, so the plan never says done for what did not happen; skip one if it does not apply.

The standard template's due days are a starting point [owner to confirm]; the 14 days for the key employment terms is the Employment Act's rule.

## What happens next

- The last day goes onto the person's record. Their annual leave is prorated to it, and **Annual leave to the last day** shows what they are entitled to, what they took and
  what is untaken, as days. What the untaken days are worth, and the final pay, come from payroll. A negative figure means the person took more than the prorated entitlement:
  the contract decides what happens next.
- The day after the last day the person's Nemura account becomes the narrow one for their own documents: see [Use your account after you leave](leaver-account.md).
- Select **Withdraw the leaving** before the last day if the person stays. Once the last day has passed, ask the Owner to correct the record.

## Common problems

- **It says there is no such task for you.** It is not yours: a joiner's task is the joiner's, a manager's the joiner's line manager's, IT's an Administrator's, and the people team may do any.
- **Start the plan is refused.** The person has a plan already, has left, or the template is archived.
- **The last day is before the person started, or notice is after the last day.** Check the dates; the message names the field.
- **The day employees were told is refused.** It is for a retrenchment only.
- **The leave settlement is missing.** The person has no legal classification, or no country pack covers the year: record the classification first.
