# Founders' money: what the company owes you, and the options

## What it is

When you pay a company cost from your own pocket, lend the company money or leave your pay
unpaid, the company owes you that amount. Finance Hub keeps it in **your director's account**, so
you can always see what the company owes you and where each amount came from.

This page is general information, not tax or legal advice. Your accountant and tax agent confirm
the choice for your situation. The platform ships with the recommended defaults below, and
Settings lets you change them.

## The recommended defaults

| Question | Recommended default | Where to change it |
|---|---|---|
| What happens to what the company owes me? | Keep it as an **interest-free loan, repayable on demand**, recorded by a board minute. At the **first priced funding round**, convert it into ordinary shares **at the round price** | Settings → Founders' money → Default settlement route |
| Should I take a salary? | **No salary**, agreed in writing before go-live and reviewed each financial year, until the company can pay you in cash. Then start a normal salary through payroll | Settings → Founders' money → Pay choice per director |
| Exception | If you hold an **Employment Pass**, its conditions may require your declared salary to be paid. Ask your accountant before choosing "no salary" | — |

Why: you keep your claim, nobody's ownership moves at an arbitrary price, there is no CPF or tax
on money you never received, and investors see a clean balance sheet at the round.

## The five ways to settle what the company owes you

| Option | What happens | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Repay | The company pays you back in cash | Not income for you. Founders are rarely repaid from a new round's money, and grant money can't be used for it |
| Keep as a loan | The amount stays owed | Investors will ask about it; it is usually converted or waived at the round |
| Convert to shares | The company issues new shares, and the debt pays for them | Needs the shareholders' approval and ACRA filings. The shares exist from the date ACRA registers them, and that date can't be backdated |
| Waive | You forgive the amount and get nothing back | Every shareholder gains in proportion to their shares. The company may owe tax if it had deducted the amount, for example unpaid pay |
| Convert to a SAFE or note | The amount turns into shares at the next priced round | Common at seed; it is included in the round's dilution |

## Your pay choices

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| No salary | Nothing to record, no CPF, nothing to convert later. The cleanest choice while cash is short |
| Salary paid | A normal payroll |
| Salary deferred | Payroll runs as usual, but your net pay is owed to you instead of paid. **CPF is still due at the end of the month, and you are taxed on the salary**, even though you received nothing. Avoid it unless your accountant recommends it |
| Director's fee | Approved by the shareholders; no CPF on fees voted at a general meeting |
| Shares instead of pay | You pay income tax on the shares' value without receiving cash. Rarely sensible before a priced round |

## Steps

1. **Paid for something yourself?** Create an expense claim, attach the receipt, and choose
   **Paid personally → Keep as a loan to the company**. Another founder approves it. To claim
   GST, the receipt or invoice must be addressed to the company.
2. **Lent the company money?** Record the bank transfer as *Money in → Loan from me*.
3. **See your balance:** Equity → *Money I put in*, or Home → the *Money I put in* card.
4. **Settle it:** Equity → *Money I put in* → **Settle**, then choose an option. The other
   founder approves it. If every founder benefits, your accountant also reviews it. Attach the
   resolutions and the agreement.
5. **Converting to shares:** after approval, your company secretary files with ACRA. Attach the
   ACRA acknowledgement. Finance Hub then posts the conversion on the date ACRA registered it.

## Common problems

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| ✖ Pay deferred without a recorded pay choice | Record the pay choice for that director and month, or pay the salary |
| ⚠ Your director's account is overdrawn | You owe the company. Repay it, or ask your accountant: loans to directors are restricted unless the company is an exempt private company |
| ⏰ Share-issue approval not filed with ACRA | File the shareholders' approval within 14 days of the resolution and attach the acknowledgement |
| ✖ Required evidence missing | Attach the resolution, agreement or ACRA acknowledgement the step lists |
