Who sees these lines

You see names, accounts and amounts because you hold the salary-payments right. A payment releaser or a controller without it sees the batch total, the number of people and the release state. Account numbers are masked for everyone; Fyniq keeps the bank-detail version from Nemura, and a newer version blocks release until the batch is approved again.
