# Use Fyniq from the keyboard

## What it is

- Keys go to each area, start each kind of record and move through a form; press **?** for every shortcut you can use, or open **Keyboard shortcuts** from the command palette.
- Shortcuts never fire while you type in a field.
- Basic mode keeps three: Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) for search and commands, Ctrl+J (⌘J) for the assistant, and **?** for the sheet; Expert mode adds the rest. See [[preferences.security]] for the modes.

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#what-it-is)

## Go to an area

- Press **G**, then the area's letter: Home (G H), Inbox (G I), Contacts (G C), Sales (G S), Purchases (G P), Expense claims (G X), Banking (G B), Reports (G R), Accounting (G A), Equity (G E), Planning (G L), Compliance (G M), Settings (G T).
- The sheet lists only the areas you can open.

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#go-to-an-area)

## Start a new record

- Press **C**, then the record's letter: C I a new invoice, C B a new bill, C J a new journal, C E a new expense claim.
- The sheet lists every kind of record your access lets you create, with its keys.

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#start-a-new-record)

## Move through a form

- In an invoice, quote, bill, purchase order, journal or expense claim, **Enter** moves to the next field; in a list open under a field, Enter still picks what is highlighted.
- **Ctrl+Enter** (⌘Enter on a Mac) saves it.

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#move-through-a-form)

## Act on selected rows

- Select rows on a page, such as bank lines in the categorise grid, then open the command palette: what you can do to them comes first, such as **Accept the selected suggestions**, the same as the page's button.

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#act-on-selected-rows)

## Turn them off or change the keys

- Press **?**, or open **Settings › Profile and security › Keyboard**, and turn **Single-character shortcuts** off; Ctrl+K and Ctrl+J still work.
- Or **Change the keys**: type one key or two for any shortcut, such as G B, and **Save the keys**; Fyniq says when two would share keys, or when one key would start another's two. **Use the usual keys** puts them back.
- The choice applies to you only; until it is saved with your profile it is kept in this browser, so set it again in another one.

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#turn-them-off-or-change-the-keys)

## The Inbox's keys

- While a request in the Inbox has the focus, **J** and **K** move, **E** opens, **A** or **R** open its approval or rejection to confirm. See [[approvals.decide]].

(src: docs/user-guide/keyboard-shortcuts.md#the-inboxs-keys)
