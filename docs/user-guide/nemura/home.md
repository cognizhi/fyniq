# Your home page in Nemura

## What it is

After you sign in, Nemura opens the home page that suits your roles. Everyone except the people team
lands on **My home**; the people team lands on **People home**. Neither shows the company's finances.

**My home** shows what a person does most as tiles: **Apply for leave**, **Ask for a letter**, **Update my details**, **Ask a question**, **My leave**
and **My requests**. Under **At a glance** it shows your annual leave left, the next public holiday you observe and how many requests are open, each with where it is.
A figure your company has not switched on, or that you may not see, is left out rather than shown as zero. Your payslip and next pay day, and Claims, appear when payroll
and the link to Fyniq are switched on.

The people team's **People home** also shows **Today**: the leave requests waiting, who is away today, certificates to verify,
flexible work requests waiting, government-paid leave with no claim filed, and what needs a look. Each is a link to the page that
deals with it; a figure you may not see shows a dash.

## Before you start

- Sign in first: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).

## Steps

1. Read **Your roles** on the page. They decide what you can see and do.
2. If it says you have no role yet, ask the Owner to give you one.
3. Select a tile for what you want to do. Your leave (see [See your leave balance](my-leave.md)), pay and letters appear here as your company switches each on.

## Common problems

- **There is nothing at this address.** The page does not exist. Select **Go to my home**.
- A role looks wrong: only the Owner changes roles. A change shows on the next page you open.
