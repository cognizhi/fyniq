# Ask the people team a question in Nemura

## What it is

- **Ask a question** sends a written question to the people team about leave, a person's record or anything else about their work.
- Someone other than the asker answers it, within 3 working days, and the answer shows under **My requests**. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/ask-hr.md#what-it-is)

## Steps

1. Open **Ask a question** and write the question, up to 1,000 characters.
2. Select **Send the question**; Nemura shows the day it is due.
3. Follow it under **Where my requests are** in [[nemura.requests]]; when it is answered its step is **Ready** and the reply is there.

(src: docs/user-guide/nemura/ask-hr.md#steps)

## What happens next

- A question is answered once; for more, ask a new question. A question nobody has answered can be withdrawn. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/ask-hr.md#what-happens-next)

## When it looks wrong

- The question is refused when it is empty or longer than 1,000 characters.
- For a letter, a change of details or flexible work use their own pages: [[nemura.letters.request]] and [[nemura.my-details]]. (src: docs/user-guide/nemura/ask-hr.md#common-problems)
