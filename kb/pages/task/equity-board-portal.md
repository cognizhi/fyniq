# Read the board packs and investor updates

## Find it

- Sign in and open the **Board and investor portal**, where a Board viewer or an investor reads the numbers.

(src: docs/user-guide/board-portal.md#find-it)

## What you see

- The investor updates sent to you: a new one is marked **New**, and opening it records that you have read it.
- The published board packs, with their figures as they were frozen when published.
- Your own holding, if you sign in as a shareholder: the shares you hold by class.
- You never see a draft pack, an update not sent to you, or anyone else's holding. A notification that says something is ready carries no figure.

(src: docs/user-guide/board-portal.md#what-you-see)

## The full cap table

- By default the viewer reads their own holding only. The whole cap table opens only while an Owner has granted it, with a reason; the portal says when it has not. An Owner can end the grant at any time, and each grant and each ending is audited.

(src: docs/user-guide/board-portal.md#the-full-cap-table)
