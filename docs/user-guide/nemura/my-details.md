# Change your own details

## What it is

**My details** is where you keep your phone, personal email, home address and up to three emergency contacts. These save at once. A change of **legal name**,
**residency** or **NRIC or FIN** is different: it needs evidence and another person on the people team to approve it. Your bank account has its own flow, with a
second approver and your passkey.

## Before you start

- Sign in first: see [Sign in to Nemura](sign-in.md).
- Your login must be linked to your person record. If it is not, Nemura says so; ask the people team.

## Steps

1. Open **My details**. Change your phone, personal email, address or postal code and select **Save my details**.
2. Under **Emergency contacts**, select **Add an emergency contact** for each person (up to three), give their name, relationship and phone, and select
   **Save my emergency contacts**. Remove one with **Remove emergency contact**.
3. To change your legal name, residency or NRIC or FIN, fill in **Ask to change my legal name, residency or NRIC or FIN**: choose what changes, give the new
   name or residency, say what changed and why, and attach the document that shows it. For a permanent resident, give the day permanent residence began.
4. Select **Send the request**. Nemura shows the day the people team decides it by.

## What happens next

- Your request appears under **Where my requests are** in [My requests](requests.md). A member of the people team who is not you reads your evidence and approves or
  refuses it, and a refusal always comes with a reason. A refused request can be sent again with better evidence.
- Approving a legal name or a residency changes your record from the day of the decision. For an NRIC or FIN, approving records that your evidence was checked, and
  the people team then records the number in the restricted fields.
- The audit trail records which of your details changed, never what they were.

## Common problems

- **Never type an NRIC or FIN number** in the note. The request holds no number; say what changed in words, such as "my FIN became an NRIC".
- **The evidence is refused as too large.** Attach a file of up to 5 MB.
- **I changed my mind.** Withdraw the request from My requests while nobody has decided it.
