How a batch is paid

  1. 1Someone prepares it from approved bills, claims and pay runs.
  2. 2A different person approves it.
  3. 3A third person, who did neither, releases it with a passkey, then pays it in the bank from the payment list.
  4. 4It is marked paid when the bank feed shows the debit.

A payee whose bank details changed after approval blocks the batch until it is approved again.
