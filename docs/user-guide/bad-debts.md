# Bad debts and GST relief

## What it is

When an invoice that bore GST is written off as a bad debt, the GST can be claimed back from IRAS in the GST return. **Sales › Bad debts** lists each such write-off with the GST in the
part written off, the first day a claim may be made and the last, and where it stands.

## The dates

- A claim may be made once twelve months have passed from the date of supply, or from the write-off when the debtor became insolvent sooner.
- It must be made within five years from the date of supply (seven for a supply before 1 January 2007), the last day being the day before the anniversary; the accountant confirms this.
- **Not yet**, **Claim now** and **Out of time** say where each debt stands. The dates come from the statutory pack, never from the page.

The GST in the part written off is the invoice's GST in proportion to the amount written off, to the cent. The claim goes in the GST return; recording it here follows.
