# Privacy notice for our customers' and suppliers' contacts

We keep the names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses of the people we deal with at our customers and
suppliers, to send and receive invoices, payments and statements, and keep them as long as the law requires our records
to be kept. Questions, or requests to see or correct your details, go to our Data Protection Officer.

## What we keep

- Your name, email address, phone number and address, as you or your company gave them to us.
- The invoices, bills, payments, statements and emails between us.
- If you are an individual customer or supplier, the bank account we pay you to, encrypted and shown masked.

## Why

[counsel to confirm] To do business with you or your company: to quote, invoice, pay and be paid, to remind you of
amounts due, and to keep the records the law requires. Business contact information given for business purposes is
outside most of the PDPA's obligations; we treat it with the same care.

## Who sees it

Our people, as their roles allow; our auditor and tax agent; and the providers who run our finance system for us under
contract, in Singapore. Emails to you go through our email provider.

## How long we keep it

Our records, including the documents we send you, at least 7 years after the end of the financial year they relate to,
as the Companies Act requires. After that, the personal details in our audit trail are destroyed. When you ask, we
remove details we no longer need for our records.

## What you can ask

[counsel to confirm] You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, and to correct it. Write to our Data
Protection Officer. We answer within 30 days, or tell you in writing within 30 days when we will.
