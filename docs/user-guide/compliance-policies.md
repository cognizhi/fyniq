# Read, acknowledge and publish the policies

## What it is

The policy library holds the company's eight policies: information security, acceptable use, access control, incident
response, backup, change management, AI use and data protection. Each policy has a version in force, which every member
reads and accepts for themselves.

It sits under **Compliance** › **Risks and policies**, below the risk register. Each policy shows its version in force,
when it was published, and how many members have acknowledged it.

## Acknowledge a policy

When a policy is published, or a new version of it, every member acknowledges it within 30 days: 30 days after it was
published, or after they joined if that was later.

1. An **Acknowledge policy** card on **Home** and in the **Inbox** lists the policies waiting for you, each with the day
   it is due. It turns to **Overdue** once that day has passed.
2. Select **Read** beside a policy. The version in force opens.
3. Read it, then select **I have read and accept it**. **Later** closes it without recording anything.

The same list is at the top of **Compliance** › **Reviews and attestations**, beside the other recurring reviews.

You acknowledge each version once, signed in as yourself. An integration or the AI assistant cannot acknowledge a
policy for you. When a new version is published, the card asks you to acknowledge that version too.

## Draft and publish a policy

The Owner and the Compliance officer keep the library.

1. Select **Draft** beside a policy. Until someone edits it, the draft is Fyniq's starting draft, written from official
   sources such as the PDPC's guides.
2. Adopt it as it is, or edit the text and select **Save the draft**. Each save is a new revision; the earlier ones are
   kept.
3. Select **Publish version** to make the draft the policy's next version.

A published version never changes: to change a policy, save a new draft and publish it. A draft that is the same as the
version in force cannot be published again. Clauses marked [counsel to confirm] bear on the law: have counsel confirm
them before you publish.

Every draft, version and acknowledgement is kept and is in the audit trail.
