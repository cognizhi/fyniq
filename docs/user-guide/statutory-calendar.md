# The statutory calendar

## What it is

**Compliance › Calendar** lists every filing and payment deadline of the company, worked out from
its financial years, its GST return periods and its settings. Fyniq files nothing itself: you file
with IRAS, CPF Board or ACRA, then record here what you did. Each item says what is due, by when and
why, in the words of the rule it comes from.

Nothing due before 7 Nov 2026, the day the calendar's rules were read, is in the calendar.

## What it holds

- **GST F5**: one month after the end of each return period, while the company is registered.
- **CPF**: by the 14th of the next month, or the next working day if the 14th is a Saturday, Sunday
  or public holiday, while the company pays staff.
- **Employment income (IR8A, AIS)**: by 1 March for the year before, while the company pays staff.
- **ECI**: within 3 months from the end of the financial year, unless waived.
- **Form C-S or C**: by 30 November of the year after the year the financial year ends in.
- **Annual return**: within seven months after the financial year's end, or eight with an overseas
  branch register.
- **AGM**: within six months after the financial year's end; or the financial statements sent to
  every member within five months, so no AGM is held.
- **Applying to register for GST**, while the GST registration monitor finds a test met.
- **The registers' own deadlines**: a controller to enter or to lodge, and share lodgements. Each opens
  its record, where it is done.

What the calendar holds follows Settings › Organisation: **Pays staff**, **Annual general meeting**
and **Keeps a branch register outside Singapore** ([Organisation settings](organisation-settings.md)).
An item whose input is missing, such as the financial year, shows **Not assessed** with what to set.

## Record a filing

1. Select **Record as filed** on the item.
2. Enter the day you filed or paid, and the acknowledgement or transaction number.
3. Attach the acknowledgement: IRAS's, CPF Board's or ACRA's, or the AGM's minutes.
4. Select **Record**.

The item shows as filed, and it leaves the reminders and Checks.

## Not required

Some items need nothing in a year. For example, ECI is waived when the year's revenue is $5 million
or below and the ECI is nil. Select **Not required**, say why, and select **Record**. No file is asked for.

## Withdraw a record

A record is never changed. If one is wrong, select **Withdraw**, say why, and record the item again.
The item is open again, and both records stay in its history.

## Reminders and Checks

Those who record filings hear of each item 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before it is due, and the day after
it passed with nothing recorded. **Checks** lists a GST return due within 7 days or past (C.2 #13),
and every other deadline due within 14 days or past (C.2 #15). The **Inbox** shows what is due in the
next 30 days.
